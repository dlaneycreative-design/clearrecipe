Bruschetta

Thick slices of grilled bread rubbed with garlic and piled high with juicy tomatoes, basil, and balsamic. The classic Italian starter that tastes like summer on a plate.

Prep 15 minsCook 8 minsServes 6

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Bruschetta, in 5 ideas

  1. Ripe tomatoes are everything; out of season, roast them first for flavor.
  2. Drain very juicy tomatoes so the bread stays crisp.
  3. Rubbing the garlic on hot bread gives flavor without harsh raw bite.
  4. Marinating the tomatoes even 15 minutes deepens every flavor.
  5. Toast the bread last so it is still warm and crisp at the table.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 rustic baguette or loaf, sliced 0.75 inch thick
  • 4 large ripe tomatoes, diced
  • 0.25 cup fresh basil leaves, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, plus 1 whole clove peeled
  • 0.25 cup extra virgin olive oil, plus more for brushing
  • 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 0.5 tsp black pepper

How to make it

Directions

  1. Toss the diced tomatoes with the basil, minced garlic, olive oil, balsamic, salt, and pepper, and let marinate 15 minutes.
  2. Brush both sides of the bread slices with olive oil and toast on a grill pan or under the broiler until golden on both sides, about 4 minutes.
  3. Rub the hot bread with the whole garlic clove so it melts into the crust.
  4. Spoon the tomato mixture generously over each slice, letting some juice soak in.
  5. Drizzle with a little more olive oil and serve immediately.
Slightly stale bread is actually ideal for bruschetta; it crisps up better and you rescue a loaf that might have gone to waste.
