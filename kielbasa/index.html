Kielbasa

Poland's famous smoked sausage: garlicky, juicy links grilled or pan-fried until snappy, served with mustard and sauerkraut.

Prep 5 minsCook 15 minsServes 4

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Kielbasa, in 5 ideas

  1. Scoring helps the fat render and the skin crisp
  2. Kielbasa is already cooked; you are just heating and browning it
  3. The apple tames the sauerkraut's sourness beautifully
  4. Beer adds malty depth to the braise
  5. Leftovers make an incredible hash with fried eggs

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 and 1/2 lbs good-quality kielbasa links
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 2 cups sauerkraut, drained
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 apple, sliced
  • 1/2 cup beer or apple cider
  • Spicy brown mustard for serving
  • Rye bread or rolls

How to make it

Directions

  1. Score the kielbasa links lightly in a crosshatch pattern.
  2. Heat the oil in a large skillet and brown the kielbasa on all sides, about 8 minutes.
  3. Push the sausage to the side and add the onion and apple. Cook 3 minutes until softening.
  4. Add the sauerkraut and beer, and simmer everything together for 5 minutes.
  5. Serve the kielbasa sliced on the bias over the sauerkraut mixture.
  6. Pass the mustard and rye bread at the table.
Money saver. Buy kielbasa from a Polish deli rather than the supermarket; the quality difference is enormous and the price is similar.
