Chicken Korma

Tender chicken braised in a mild, creamy cashew and yogurt sauce, gentle enough for spice beginners.

Prep 20 mins plus marinatingCook 35 minsServes 4
#Indian#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Chicken Korma, in 5 ideas

  1. Soaking cashews makes them blend silky smooth.
  2. Frying the onion first adds a deep sweetness.
  3. Keep the heat gentle once yogurt is in to prevent curdling.
  4. Korma should be mild and creamy, not fiery.
  5. Garnish with toasted almonds for crunch.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs chicken thighs, cubed
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 1/2 cup cashews, soaked
  • 1 onion, sliced and fried golden
  • 4 cloves garlic and 1 inch ginger
  • 1 tsp garam masala and 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric and 1/2 tsp cardamom powder
  • 3 tbsp ghee
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 cup cream

How to make it

Directions

  1. Marinate chicken in half the yogurt, salt, and half the spices for 1 hour.
  2. Blend cashews, fried onion, garlic, ginger, and remaining yogurt into a smooth paste.
  3. Sear chicken in ghee until golden; remove.
  4. Add the blended paste to the pan and cook until the oil separates.
  5. Return chicken with 1 cup water and simmer 20 minutes.
  6. Stir in cream and garam masala; serve with basmati rice.
Money saver. Cashews from the bulk bin are far cheaper than small bags.
