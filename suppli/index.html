Suppli

Roman fried risotto balls with a molten mozzarella heart that stretches when you pull them apart. Street food perfection.

Prep 20 minsCook 30 minsServes 8
#Italian#Starters

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Suppli, in 5 ideas

  1. Cold risotto is essential; warm rice will not hold its shape.
  2. The mozzarella center should be completely sealed inside or it leaks into the oil.
  3. Chilling the breaded balls keeps the coating from sliding off in the fryer.
  4. Leftover plain risotto is the traditional starting point; this is a great use for it.
  5. In Rome they are called 'suppli al telefono' because the cheese stretches like telephone cords.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked risotto, cooled (or 1 cup arborio rice cooked in broth)
  • 4 oz mozzarella, cut into 8 cubes
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 1/2 cups breadcrumbs
  • 4 cups vegetable oil, for frying
  • 1/2 cup marinara sauce, warmed, for serving
  • Salt to taste

How to make it

Directions

  1. Wet your hands and shape the cold risotto into 8 oval balls, pressing a mozzarella cube into the center of each and sealing it well.
  2. Roll each ball in flour, then beaten egg, then breadcrumbs. Chill 20 minutes so the coating sets.
  3. Heat the oil to 350F in a deep pot.
  4. Fry the suppli in batches, 3 to 4 minutes, turning once, until deep golden.
  5. Drain on a wire rack, sprinkle with salt, and serve hot with warm marinara.
Money saver. Make a big batch and freeze the breaded, unfried balls. Fry straight from frozen, adding 2 minutes.
