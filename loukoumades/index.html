Loukoumades

Golden puffs of fried dough soaked in honey syrup with cinnamon and walnuts. Greece's oldest dessert, still the best.

Prep 20 mins plus 1 hr riseCook 20 minsServes 6

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Loukoumades, in 5 ideas

  1. The batter should be thick and elastic, almost like bread dough.
  2. Keep the oil at 170C, too cool and they soak up grease.
  3. Drain them well before the honey bath.
  4. Warm honey syrup soaks in better than cold.
  5. Crushed walnuts and cinnamon are the classic finish.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 250g flour
  • 7g instant yeast
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 200ml warm water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Oil, for frying
  • 200g honey
  • 100ml water
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 50g walnuts, crushed

How to make it

Directions

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar, salt and warm water into a thick batter. Cover and rise 1 hour until doubled.
  2. Heat oil to 170C (340F).
  3. Drop spoonfuls of batter into the oil and fry, turning, until deep golden, about 3 minutes per batch.
  4. Drain on a rack.
  5. Warm the honey with 100ml water, pour over the hot puffs, and dust with cinnamon and walnuts.
Money saver. A deep pot of oil fries a huge batch, making this the cheapest crowd dessert you can make.
