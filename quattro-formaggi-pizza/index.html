Quattro Formaggi Pizza

Four cheeses melt together into one rich, creamy white pizza with no tomato sauce in sight: mozzarella, gorgonzola, fontina, and Parmesan over a golden crust with a drizzle of honey.

Prep 30 minsCook 12 minsServes 4
#Italian#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Quattro Formaggi Pizza, in 5 ideas

  1. No tomato sauce lets the four cheeses be the star, so use good cheese.
  2. Honey on a savory cheese pizza sounds odd and tastes amazing.
  3. Gorgonzola melts fast, so scatter it last and use it sparingly.
  4. A garlic oil base gives the white pizza the flavor the missing sauce would.
  5. Let it rest 2 minutes so the cheese sets before slicing.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 lb pizza dough
  • 1 cup shredded whole milk mozzarella
  • 0.75 cup shredded fontina
  • 0.5 cup crumbled gorgonzola
  • 0.33 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp honey, for serving
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves

How to make it

Directions

  1. Place a pizza stone or baking sheet in the oven and heat to 500°F for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Stretch the dough into a 14 inch round on a floured surface, keeping a slightly thicker rim.
  3. Move the dough to a floured peel or parchment and brush all over with olive oil mixed with the minced garlic.
  4. Scatter the mozzarella and fontina first, then dot with gorgonzola and Parmesan.
  5. Slide the pizza onto the hot stone and bake 10 to 12 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheeses are bubbling and spotty brown.
  6. Finish with thyme leaves and a thin drizzle of honey, then slice and serve immediately.
Buying small wedges from a deli counter costs less than full blocks when you only need half a cup of each cheese.
