Antipasto Platter

Cured meats, cheeses, olives, and marinated vegetables arranged for grazing. The easiest impressive starter you will ever make.

Prep 20 minsCook 0 minsServes 8
#Italian#Starters

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Antipasto Platter, in 5 ideas

  1. Let cheeses come to room temperature for the best flavor.
  2. Fold cured meats into ribbons or rosettes for a professional look.
  3. Work in odd numbers of clusters for a natural arrangement.
  4. Leave small gaps, a crowded board looks abundant and inviting.
  5. Refill crackers and bread as guests graze.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 4 oz sliced prosciutto
  • 4 oz sliced salami
  • 4 oz sliced mortadella
  • 8 oz fresh mozzarella balls
  • 6 oz aged Parmesan, cut into chunks
  • 1 cup mixed olives
  • 1 cup marinated artichoke hearts
  • 1 cup roasted red peppers
  • 1/2 cup cornichons
  • 1/4 cup pepperoncini
  • Crackers and sliced baguette
  • Olive oil and balsamic for drizzling

How to make it

Directions

  1. Choose a large board, platter, or sheet pan as your base.
  2. Place small bowls of olives, artichokes, and cornichons around the board.
  3. Fold or roll the prosciutto, salami, and mortadella and arrange in clusters.
  4. Add the mozzarella balls and Parmesan chunks in the gaps.
  5. Tuck in the roasted red peppers and pepperoncini.
  6. Fill remaining spaces with crackers and baguette slices.
  7. Drizzle the mozzarella with olive oil and balsamic.
  8. Serve immediately at room temperature.
Buy deli ends and odd cuts for the meats, they taste the same arranged on a board and cost half as much.
