Cabbage Rolls

Golabki: tender cabbage leaves wrapped around pork and rice, braised in tomato sauce until melting and sweet.

Prep 40 minsCook 1 hour 30 minsServes 6
#Polish#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Cabbage Rolls, in 5 ideas

  1. Blanching the whole head makes the leaves pliable without tearing
  2. Trimming the thick rib lets the leaves roll tightly
  3. The brown sugar balances the tomato sauce the Polish way
  4. Line the pot with torn leaves to prevent scorching
  5. They reheat beautifully and freeze well

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 large head green cabbage
  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 1/2 lb ground beef
  • 1 cup cooked rice
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon marjoram
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar

How to make it

Directions

  1. Core the cabbage and blanch the whole head in boiling salted water for 5 minutes. Peel off the softened outer leaves and repeat until you have 12 large leaves. Trim the thick ribs.
  2. Mix the pork, beef, rice, onion, egg, salt, pepper, and marjoram.
  3. Place a scoop of filling on each leaf, fold in the sides, and roll up snugly.
  4. Line a heavy pot with the torn inner cabbage leaves and arrange the rolls seam-side down.
  5. Whisk the tomato sauce, broth, and brown sugar and pour over the rolls.
  6. Cover and braise at 325 F for 90 minutes until the cabbage is silky and the filling is cooked through.
Money saver. Use the whole cabbage; the inner leaves you do not roll become the protective pot lining, so nothing is wasted.
