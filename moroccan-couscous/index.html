Moroccan Couscous

Fluffy steamed couscous piled with tender vegetables and chickpeas in a fragrant broth.

Prep 20 minsCook 40 minsServes 6
#Moroccan#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Moroccan Couscous, in 5 ideas

  1. Fluff the couscous twice for the lightest texture.
  2. Cut vegetables chunky so they survive the simmer.
  3. Ras el hanout is the key spice blend.
  4. Serve the broth on the side for drizzling.
  5. A fried egg on top is a common addition.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 2 cups couscous
  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 carrots, 2 zucchini, and 1 turnip, chunked
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 2 tomatoes, quartered
  • 2 tsp ras el hanout, 1 tsp turmeric, and 1 tsp ginger powder
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Harissa to serve

How to make it

Directions

  1. Simmer vegetables, chickpeas, tomatoes, spices, and salt in broth until tender, about 30 minutes.
  2. Meanwhile, pour boiling broth over couscous, cover, and steam 10 minutes; fluff with a fork.
  3. Drizzle couscous with olive oil and fluff again.
  4. Mound couscous on a platter and arrange vegetables on top.
  5. Ladle over some broth and serve harissa on the side.
Money saver. Couscous is one of the fastest, cheapest grains to prepare.
