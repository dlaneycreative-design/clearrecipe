Quesadillas

Golden crisp tortillas with molten cheese pulling into long strings. Simple, fast, and universally loved.

Prep 10 minsCook 15 minsServes 4

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Quesadillas, in 5 ideas

  1. Medium heat is the sweet spot. Too hot burns the tortilla before the cheese melts.
  2. Grate your own cheese. It melts into strings instead of a greasy sheet.
  3. Do not overfill. A thin even layer melts better and stays inside.
  4. A lid on the skillet for the first minute traps heat and melts the cheese faster.
  5. Let them rest a minute so the cheese sets slightly and does not run everywhere.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 8 large flour tortillas
  • 3 cups Monterey Jack or Oaxaca cheese, grated
  • 1 cup cooked shredded chicken (optional)
  • 1/2 cup sauteed peppers and onions (optional)
  • 2 tbsp butter or oil
  • Sour cream, guacamole, and salsa, for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Heat a large skillet over medium heat with a thin film of butter or oil.
  2. Lay a tortilla flat and spread cheese over half. Add chicken or veggies if using.
  3. Fold the tortilla in half. Cook 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden and the cheese melts.
  4. Repeat with remaining tortillas, adding butter as needed.
  5. Rest 1 minute, then cut into wedges.
  6. Serve with sour cream, guacamole, and salsa.
Quesadillas are the ultimate leftover vehicle. Any cooked meat or roasted veg works tucked inside.
