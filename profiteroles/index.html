Profiteroles

Crisp choux puffs filled with vanilla ice cream and drowned in warm chocolate sauce. Pure indulgence.

Prep 25 minsCook 35 minsServes 8
#French#Desserts

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Profiteroles, in 5 ideas

  1. Choux pastry is steam-leavened; opening the oven early collapses the puffs.
  2. The dough should be glossy and fall slowly from the spoon in a V shape.
  3. Poke the steam hole while they are hot or the insides go soggy.
  4. Fill just before serving so the shells stay crisp.
  5. Leftover unfilled puffs freeze beautifully for a month.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 4 large eggs
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 pint vanilla ice cream
  • 6 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp butter

How to make it

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 400F. Boil the water, butter, and salt in a saucepan. Dump in the flour all at once and stir until a smooth ball forms and pulls from the sides, 2 minutes.
  2. Cool 5 minutes, then beat in the eggs one at a time until glossy and pipeable.
  3. Pipe 16 mounds onto a parchment-lined sheet. Bake 25 minutes until puffed and deep golden. Do not open the oven early.
  4. Poke a small hole in the bottom of each puff to release steam, then cool completely.
  5. For the sauce, heat the cream until steaming, pour over the chocolate and butter, and stir smooth.
  6. Split each puff, fill with a scoop of ice cream, and spoon warm chocolate sauce over.
Money saver. A zip-top bag with the corner snipped works as a piping bag. No fancy tips needed for rustic puffs.
