Penne Arrabbiata

Penne tossed in a fiery tomato sauce spiked with garlic and red pepper flakes. Arrabbiata means angry, and this one has real attitude.

Prep 10 minsCook 20 minsServes 4
#Italian#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Penne Arrabbiata, in 5 ideas

  1. Blooming the pepper flakes in oil before the tomatoes go in is what gives arrabbiata its deep, even heat.
  2. A pinch of sugar tames canned tomato acidity without making the sauce sweet.
  3. Penne rigate, the kind with ridges, grabs this sauce far better than smooth penne.
  4. Let the garlic go golden but never brown, burned garlic turns the whole sauce bitter.
  5. Start with 1 tsp of flakes and add more, you can always add heat, you cannot take it back.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 12 oz penne
  • 1 28-oz can crushed tomatoes
  • 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt to taste
  • 1/4 cup torn fresh basil leaves
  • Grated Pecorino Romano, for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the sliced garlic and red pepper flakes and cook, stirring, until the garlic is golden at the edges, about 2 minutes.
  2. Pour in the crushed tomatoes, add the sugar and 1 tsp salt, and bring to a simmer.
  3. Cook the sauce uncovered for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it thickens slightly and the oil starts to separate at the edges.
  4. Meanwhile, boil the penne in well-salted water until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water, then drain.
  5. Add the penne to the sauce with a splash of pasta water and toss until every piece is coated.
  6. Finish with the torn basil and serve with Pecorino Romano on top.
Canned crushed tomatoes go on sale for under a dollar a can, stock the pantry and this becomes a five-dollar dinner for four.
