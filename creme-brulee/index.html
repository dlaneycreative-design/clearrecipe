Creme Brulee

Silky vanilla bean custard with a glass thin caramelized sugar crust that cracks under your spoon. Six ingredients, one torch, pure elegance.

Prep 15 minsCook 40 mins plus chillingServes 6
#French#Desserts

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Creme Brulee, in 5 ideas

  1. The water bath is essential, it keeps the custard silky instead of rubbery.
  2. Strain the custard to catch any bits of cooked egg for a perfectly smooth finish.
  3. A kitchen torch gives the thinnest, most even crust, the broiler is uneven and risky.
  4. Torch just before serving, the crust softens after about an hour in the fridge.
  5. Pull them when the center still jiggles slightly, the custard finishes setting as it chills.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 vanilla bean, split and scraped
  • 5 large egg yolks
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar, plus more for the tops
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 6 tsp superfine sugar, for torching

How to make it

Directions

  1. Heat the cream with the vanilla bean and seeds until steaming, then steep 20 minutes off the heat. Remove the pod.
  2. Whisk the yolks, 1/2 cup sugar, and salt until pale, then slowly whisk in the warm cream.
  3. Strain the custard into a pitcher, then divide among 6 ramekins set in a roasting pan.
  4. Pour hot water into the pan to come halfway up the ramekins and bake at 300F for 35 to 40 minutes until the edges are set with a gentle wobble in the center.
  5. Cool, then refrigerate at least 4 hours or overnight.
  6. Before serving, sprinkle 1 tsp sugar over each custard and torch until melted and amber. Rest 2 minutes so the crust hardens.
Save the leftover egg whites in the freezer for meringues or omelets, they keep for months and you never pay for waste.
