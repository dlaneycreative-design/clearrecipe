Full English Breakfast

The classic plateful that powers the nation: crispy bacon, plump sausages, a perfectly fried egg and all the trimmings, ready to set you up for the day.

Prep 10 minsCook 25 minsServes 2
#British#Breakfasts

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Full English Breakfast, in 5 ideas

  1. Timing is everything, so get the sausages and bacon in the pan first and let everything else land while they finish.
  2. A proper fried egg wants hot oil and a spoon to baste the white over the yolk until it just sets.
  3. Black pudding is the divisive hero here: crisp the slices hard and doubters tend to come around.
  4. Warm your plates in a low oven so nothing cools down while you plate the rest.
  5. Beans on the plate or in a ramekin on the side is a personal choice, but nobody wants a cold bean.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 4 pork sausages (about 400g)
  • 4 rashers smoked back bacon
  • 2 large free-range eggs
  • 200g tinned baked beans
  • 2 medium tomatoes, halved
  • 4 slices black pudding (about 150g)
  • 200g button mushrooms, halved
  • 2 slices thick white bread or 1 bloomer loaf slice each
  • 30g butter
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • HP sauce or ketchup, to serve

How to make it

Directions

  1. Put a large heavy frying pan over medium heat with the oil and add the sausages. Cook gently for 15 minutes, turning regularly, until browned all over and cooked through.
  2. Move the sausages to the edges of the pan and add the bacon, mushrooms and black pudding slices. Fry for 3 to 4 minutes, turning the bacon once and flipping the black pudding until crisp.
  3. Push everything to one side, add the butter, and crack in the eggs. Spoon hot fat over the whites until they set, then season with salt and pepper.
  4. Meanwhile warm the beans in a small saucepan over low heat and grill or fry the tomato halves until softened.
  5. Toast the bread and butter it while hot.
  6. Plate everything up, spoon the beans alongside, season the tomatoes, and serve immediately with HP sauce or ketchup.
Buy the sausages from a proper butcher when they are on offer and freeze them in pairs; a good sausage is the difference between an average breakfast and a great one.
