Lamb Kofta

Hand formed spiced lamb skewers, juicy and charred, with warm spices in every bite.

Prep 15 minsCook 15 minsServes 4
#Middle Eastern#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Lamb Kofta, in 5 ideas

  1. Grated onion keeps the kofta moist.
  2. Chilling before grilling prevents falling apart.
  3. Do not overmix or the texture turns dense.
  4. A touch of cinnamon is the secret background note.
  5. Serve with a sharp salad to cut the richness.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs ground lamb
  • 1 onion, grated and squeezed
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup chopped parsley
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne
  • Flatbreads and tahini to serve

How to make it

Directions

  1. Mix all ingredients gently until just combined; do not overwork.
  2. Form into long oval kebabs around skewers or into patties.
  3. Chill 20 minutes to firm up.
  4. Grill over medium high heat, turning, until charred and cooked through, about 10 minutes.
  5. Rest 5 minutes and serve with flatbreads and tahini.
Money saver. Ground lamb is often cheapest at Middle Eastern butchers.
