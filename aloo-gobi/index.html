Aloo Gobi

A humble, comforting dry curry of potatoes and cauliflower tossed with turmeric and warm spices.

Prep 15 minsCook 25 minsServes 4
#Indian#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Aloo Gobi, in 5 ideas

  1. Keep the pieces chunky so they hold their shape.
  2. This is a dry curry, so let the water evaporate at the end.
  3. Frozen cauliflower works in a pinch; add it straight from frozen.
  4. A squeeze of lemon lifts the earthy spices.
  5. Great tucked into wraps for lunch the next day.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 3 potatoes, cubed
  • 1 small cauliflower, cut in florets
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds and 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp coriander, and 1/2 tsp cayenne
  • 1 inch ginger, grated
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 cup chopped cilantro

How to make it

Directions

  1. Heat oil and crackle cumin and mustard seeds.
  2. Add onion and cook until soft and golden.
  3. Stir in ginger, turmeric, coriander, cayenne, and salt.
  4. Add potatoes and 1/4 cup water; cover and cook 10 minutes.
  5. Add cauliflower and tomatoes; cover and cook until tender, about 12 minutes.
  6. Uncover and cook off excess moisture, then shower with cilantro.
Money saver. One of the cheapest curries to make; potatoes and cauliflower are always affordable.
