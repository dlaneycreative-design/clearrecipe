Leberkase

Bavaria's beloved meatloaf: finely ground pork and beef baked into a golden loaf, sliced thick and served hot in a crusty roll.

Prep 15 minsCook 1 hour 15 minsServes 8
#German#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Leberkase, in 5 ideas

  1. Keeping everything ice cold is the key to the smooth, fine texture
  2. The food processor step is what separates leberkase from regular meatloaf
  3. The diamond scoring is traditional and helps the crust crisp
  4. Serve it warm; it is at its best fresh from the oven
  5. Leftover slices fry up beautifully for breakfast

What you need

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs ground pork
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 cup ice water
  • 2 tablespoons salt
  • 1 teaspoon white pepper
  • 1 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1 teaspoon marjoram
  • 1 onion, very finely minced
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • Crusty rolls and sweet mustard for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Chill all the meat and the bowl for 30 minutes; everything must stay cold.
  2. Working in batches, process the pork, beef, onion, and ice water in a food processor until completely smooth and pasty.
  3. Mix in the salt, pepper, nutmeg, marjoram, and cornstarch until evenly distributed.
  4. Pack firmly into a greased 9 by 5 loaf pan, smoothing the top. Score a shallow diamond pattern on top.
  5. Bake at 350 F for 75 minutes until deeply golden with a crisp crust and the internal temperature reaches 160 F.
  6. Rest 15 minutes, slice thick, and serve hot in crusty rolls with sweet mustard.
Money saver. Ask your butcher for coarse ground pork shoulder; it is cheaper than pre-packaged and grinds more smoothly.
