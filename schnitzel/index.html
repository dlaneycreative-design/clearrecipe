Schnitzel

Pounded cutlets breaded and fried golden, served with lemon. Austria and Germany's most beloved plate.

Prep 15 minsCook 15 minsServes 4
#German#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Schnitzel, in 5 ideas

  1. Pound the cutlets evenly thin, about 5mm.
  2. The classic breading is flour, egg, then breadcrumbs, in that order.
  3. Fry in plenty of fat so it crisps instead of steaming.
  4. Do not press it in the pan, let it float.
  5. Lemon is the only sauce it needs.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 4 pork or veal cutlets
  • 100g flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 150g breadcrumbs
  • 100g butter or lard
  • 1 lemon, in wedges
  • Salt and pepper
  • Parsley

How to make it

Directions

  1. Pound the cutlets to 5mm thick between plastic wrap and season.
  2. Set up three plates: flour, beaten egg, breadcrumbs.
  3. Coat each cutlet in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs, pressing gently.
  4. Fry in hot butter 3 to 4 minutes per side until deep golden.
  5. Drain briefly, serve with lemon wedges and parsley.
Money saver. Pork loin cutlets cost a fraction of veal and make an excellent schnitzel.
