Cinnamon Buns

Swedish kanelbullar: cardamom-scented soft buns swirled with cinnamon butter, knotted and sprinkled with pearl sugar.

Prep 40 mins plus risingCook 12 minsServes 12
#Scandinavian#Bakings

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Cinnamon Buns, in 5 ideas

  1. Cardamom in the dough is what makes them Swedish, not American
  2. The knotted shape looks impressive but is just a twisted strip tucked under
  3. Do not overbake; they should be soft and just barely golden
  4. Pearl sugar does not melt, giving the classic crunchy topping
  5. Serve warm with coffee for a proper Swedish fika

What you need

Ingredients

  • 3 and 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 and 1/4 teaspoons instant yeast
  • 1 teaspoon ground cardamom
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup warm milk
  • 1/3 cup soft butter
  • For the filling: 1/2 cup soft butter, 1/2 cup brown sugar, 2 tablespoons cinnamon
  • Pearl sugar and 1 beaten egg for finishing

How to make it

Directions

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, yeast, cardamom, and salt. Add the warm milk and butter and knead 8 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  2. Let rise in a warm spot for 1 hour until doubled.
  3. Roll into a large rectangle. Spread with the cinnamon butter filling.
  4. Fold in thirds like a letter, cut into strips, and twist each strip into a knot.
  5. Place on lined baking sheets, cover, and proof 30 minutes.
  6. Brush with beaten egg, sprinkle with pearl sugar, and bake at 425 F for 10 to 12 minutes until golden.
Money saver. Make a double batch and freeze the baked buns; they reheat perfectly in a warm oven.
