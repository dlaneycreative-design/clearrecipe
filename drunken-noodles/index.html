Drunken Noodles

Fiery wide rice noodles with Thai basil, bell peppers, and chicken. Spicy, aromatic, and unapologetically bold.

Prep 20 minsCook 10 minsServes 4
#Thai#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Drunken Noodles, in 5 ideas

  1. Thai basil is essential, Italian basil is a different herb entirely
  2. Control the heat with the number of chilies, the seeds hold the fire
  3. The basil goes in off the heat so it stays vibrant and aromatic
  4. Fish sauce brings the funky salty depth, do not substitute soy sauce alone
  5. This is meant to be eaten immediately while the noodles are still smoky

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 lb fresh wide rice noodles
  • 3/4 lb chicken thighs, sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 Thai chilies, minced
  • 1 cup Thai basil leaves
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tsp sugar

How to make it

Directions

  1. Mix the oyster sauce, fish sauce, soy sauce, and sugar.
  2. Sear the chicken in hot oil 3 minutes. Add garlic and chilies and fry 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add the onion and bell pepper and stir fry 2 minutes.
  4. Add the noodles and sauce. Toss over high heat 3 minutes until charred in spots.
  5. Kill the heat and fold in the Thai basil until just wilted. Serve immediately.
Money saver. Thai basil grows easily in a pot on a windowsill, saving you the specialty store markup.
