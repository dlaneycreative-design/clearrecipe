Calamares a la Romana

Roman style fried calamari rings in a light batter with lemon. Crispy, tender, and dangerously snackable.

Prep 15 minsCook 10 minsServes 4
#Spanish#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Calamares a la Romana, in 5 ideas

  1. The batter should be thin; you want a crisp shell, not a doughy coat
  2. Sparkling water makes the batter lighter than still water
  3. Two minutes is the sweet spot; longer and the squid turns rubbery
  4. Patting the squid dry is what keeps the batter sticking
  5. Serve immediately; fried calamari waits for no one

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs squid, cleaned and cut into rings
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 cup cold sparkling water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Oil, for frying
  • Lemon wedges and aioli, for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Pat the squid very dry.
  2. Whisk flour, baking powder, salt, and sparkling water into a thin batter.
  3. Heat oil to 360 F.
  4. Dip rings in batter, let excess drip off, and fry 2 minutes until golden.
  5. Drain on a rack and salt immediately.
  6. Serve with lemon wedges and aioli.
Money saver. Buy whole squid and clean it yourself; it is half the price of pre cut rings and takes only minutes.
