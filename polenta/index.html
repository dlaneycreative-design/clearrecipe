Polenta

Creamy stone ground cornmeal, buttery and smooth, with a crisp edge if you like. The perfect bed for braises and stews.

Prep 5 minsCook 40 minsServes 4

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Polenta, in 5 ideas

  1. Pour the polenta in slowly while whisking to prevent lumps.
  2. A heavy pot stops the bottom from scorching during the long cook.
  3. It is done when it pulls away from the sides of the pot.
  4. Stir in mascarpone instead of Parmesan for extra richness.
  5. Leftover polenta fries up beautifully the next day.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 cup coarse stone ground polenta
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  • Black pepper to taste
  • Fresh herbs for serving (optional)

How to make it

Directions

  1. Bring the water and salt to a boil in a heavy pot.
  2. Whisk in the polenta in a slow, steady stream to avoid lumps.
  3. Lower the heat to a gentle simmer and stir frequently.
  4. Cook 35 to 40 minutes, stirring often, until thick and the grains are tender.
  5. Beat in the butter and Parmesan until creamy.
  6. Season with black pepper and more salt if needed.
  7. Spoon into bowls and top with fresh herbs if you like.
  8. For crisp polenta, spread in a pan, chill, slice, and pan fry.
Coarse stone ground polenta costs a little more than instant but the texture is worth every penny.
