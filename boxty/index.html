Boxty

Crisp Irish potato pancakes, golden outside and fluffy inside. Breakfast, lunch, or a midnight snack.

Prep 15 minsCook 20 minsServes 4
#British#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Boxty, in 5 ideas

  1. Squeeze the grated potato dry for the crispiest result.
  2. A mix of raw and mashed potato gives the best texture.
  3. Medium heat, they need time to cook through.
  4. Keep them warm in a low oven while you fry batches.
  5. Sour cream and chives are the classic topping.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 500g potatoes, half grated, half mashed
  • 100g flour
  • 1 egg
  • 100ml buttermilk
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • Butter, for frying
  • Salt and pepper

How to make it

Directions

  1. Squeeze the grated potato in a cloth to remove excess liquid.
  2. Mix with the mashed potato, flour, egg, buttermilk, baking soda, salt and pepper.
  3. Heat butter in a pan over medium heat.
  4. Drop in spoonfuls and flatten, frying 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden and cooked through.
  5. Serve hot with sour cream.
Money saver. This is a leftover potato recipe at heart, use up what you have.
