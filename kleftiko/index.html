Kleftiko

Lamb and potatoes sealed in parchment and slow roasted until they fall apart. The bandit's dinner, and worth the wait.

Prep 20 minsCook 3 hrsServes 4
#Greek#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Kleftiko, in 5 ideas

  1. The parchment seal steams everything in its own juices.
  2. Lamb shoulder has the fat and flavor this needs, leg dries out.
  3. Do not peek for the first 2 hours, the seal matters.
  4. A squeeze of lemon at the end wakes everything up.
  5. It reheats beautifully, make extra.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1kg lamb shoulder, bone in, cut in chunks
  • 600g potatoes, in wedges
  • 1 whole garlic head, cloves separated
  • 2 tbsp dried oregano
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 100ml olive oil
  • 2 tomatoes, quartered
  • Salt and pepper

How to make it

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 160C (320F). Toss the lamb, potatoes, garlic, tomatoes, oregano, lemon, olive oil, salt and pepper in a roasting pan.
  2. Cover the pan tightly with a double layer of parchment, then foil, sealing the edges.
  3. Roast 2 hours 30 minutes without opening.
  4. Remove the cover and roast 30 minutes more until browned.
  5. Serve straight from the pan with the juices.
Money saver. Lamb shoulder is the cheapest lamb cut and the most flavorful for slow cooking.
