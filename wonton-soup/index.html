Wonton Soup

Delicate pork and shrimp wontons floating in a clear ginger scented broth with bok choy. Light, warming, and endlessly slurpable.

Prep 30 minsCook 15 minsServes 4
#Chinese#Soups

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Wonton Soup, in 5 ideas

  1. A sticky filling that holds together comes from mixing in one direction until tacky
  2. Press out air pockets when sealing or the wontons puff and burst
  3. Wontons float when they are nearly done, give them one more minute after
  4. White pepper gives the classic gentle heat of restaurant wonton soup
  5. Freeze extra wontons on a tray for instant soup any night

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1/2 lb ground pork
  • 1/4 lb shrimp, minced
  • 2 green onions, minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 24 wonton wrappers
  • 6 cups chicken stock
  • 2 cups baby bok choy
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Salt and white pepper

How to make it

Directions

  1. Mix the pork, shrimp, green onions, ginger, soy sauce, and sesame oil into a sticky filling.
  2. Place a teaspoon of filling in each wrapper, wet the edges, and fold into triangles, pressing out air.
  3. Simmer the stock with soy sauce, sesame oil, salt, and white pepper.
  4. Add the wontons and simmer 4 to 5 minutes until they float and the filling is cooked.
  5. Add the bok choy for the last 2 minutes. Serve hot.
Money saver. A pack of wonton wrappers is cheap and this makes two dozen. Freeze half for a future meal.
