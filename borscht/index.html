Borscht

Ruby-red beet soup with cabbage, carrots, and potatoes, finished with a swirl of sour cream. Eastern Europe's sweetest comfort bowl.

Prep 20 minsCook 50 minsServes 6
#Ukraine#Starters

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Borscht, in 5 ideas

  1. Grate, do not chop, the beets. Grated beets release their color and cook faster. Chunks stay hard and pale.
  2. Serve it cold in summer. Chilled borscht is a completely different and wonderful dish. Add extra dill and a squeeze of lemon.
  3. Beef stock adds depth. Vegetable stock works fine, but a rich beef stock makes it taste like it simmered all day.
  4. It improves overnight. Like most soups, the flavors marry in the fridge. Reheat gently, do not boil hard.
  5. Rye bread is the classic partner. Dark, dense, slightly sour. Made for dunking.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 3 medium beets, peeled and grated
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 3 cups shredded green cabbage
  • 2 potatoes, diced
  • 6 cups beef or vegetable stock
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Sour cream and fresh dill, for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook the onion 5 minutes until soft. Add the carrots and cook 3 minutes more.
  2. Stir in the grated beets and tomato paste. Cook 5 minutes, stirring, until the beets soften slightly and stain everything red.
  3. Pour in the stock. Add the cabbage, potatoes, vinegar, and sugar. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer.
  4. Cook 35 to 40 minutes until the potatoes are tender and the cabbage is silky. The soup should be a deep crimson.
  5. Season with salt and pepper. Taste for balance: it should be earthy, a little sweet, a little sour. Adjust vinegar and sugar.
  6. Ladle into bowls and serve with a generous spoonful of sour cream and a shower of fresh dill.
The vinegar protects the color. Acid keeps beets brilliantly red. Without it the soup turns a dull brownish purple.
