Gumbo

Dark roux based stew with chicken, andouille, and okra, served over rice. The soul of Louisiana in a bowl.

Prep 30 minsCook 2 hrsServes 8
#American#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Gumbo, in 5 ideas

  1. The roux is everything. Dark chocolate color means deep nutty flavor. Do not rush it and do not walk away.
  2. A flat wooden spatula scrapes the corners where roux burns first.
  3. Okra thickens the gumbo naturally as it cooks down.
  4. Filé powder goes on at the table, never in the pot, or it gets stringy.
  5. Gumbo should coat a spoon but still be soupy enough to ladle over rice.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 cup vegetable oil and 1 cup flour (for roux)
  • 1 onion, 1 bell pepper, and 2 celery stalks, all diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 lb andouille sausage, sliced
  • 1.5 lbs chicken thighs
  • 6 cups chicken broth
  • 2 cups sliced okra
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning mixed with 1 tsp thyme and 1/2 tsp cayenne
  • 4 green onions, sliced
  • Cooked white rice and file powder for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Make the roux: heat oil in a heavy Dutch oven over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and cook, stirring constantly, 30 to 45 minutes until the color of dark chocolate.
  2. Add the onion, bell pepper, and celery to the hot roux. Cook 8 minutes, stirring, until soft. Add the garlic, cook 1 minute.
  3. Slowly whisk in the broth. Add the sausage, chicken, okra, bay leaves, Cajun seasoning, thyme, and cayenne.
  4. Bring to a boil, then simmer on low 1.5 hours until the chicken shreds easily.
  5. Remove the chicken, shred it, and return it to the pot.
  6. Skim excess fat. Serve over rice, topped with green onions and a pinch of filé if using.
Make the roux in the oven instead of on the stovetop: whisk oil and flour in a Dutch oven and bake at 350F for 90 minutes, stirring every 20. No arm workout required.
