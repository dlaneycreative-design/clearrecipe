Duck a l Orange

Crispy roasted duck with a glossy bittersweet orange sauce. A French classic that earns its reputation.

Prep 20 minsCook 1 hr 45 minsServes 4
#French#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Duck a l Orange, in 5 ideas

  1. Pricking the skin lets the fat render out. That is the whole game.
  2. Drain the fat twice during roasting for the crispiest skin.
  3. Caramelize the sugar slowly. Burned sugar cannot be saved.
  4. Rest the duck before carving, always.
  5. Save the rendered duck fat. It is liquid gold for potatoes.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 whole duck (about 5 lbs)
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 cup orange juice, fresh
  • Zest of 2 oranges
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup red wine vinegar
  • 1 1/2 cups chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp Grand Marnier (optional)
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Orange segments, for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Prick the duck skin all over with a fork, rub with salt and pepper, and roast at 350F breast side up for about 1 hour 45 minutes, draining fat twice.
  2. Meanwhile, caramelize the sugar in a saucepan until amber. Add the vinegar carefully and stir.
  3. Add the orange juice, zest, and stock. Simmer 15 minutes until syrupy.
  4. Whisk in the butter and Grand Marnier. Season to taste.
  5. Rest the duck 15 minutes, carve, and spoon the sauce over.
  6. Serve with orange segments.
Money saver. A whole duck feeds four generously and costs less per pound than duck breasts. The leftover fat is a bonus.
