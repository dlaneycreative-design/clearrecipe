Eggplant Rollatini

Thin roasted eggplant slices rolled around creamy ricotta, baked in marinara. Lighter than lasagna, just as comforting.

Prep 25 minsCook 35 minsServes 4
#Italian#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Eggplant Rollatini, in 5 ideas

  1. Salting the eggplant first keeps it from going soggy.
  2. Roast, do not fry. Same flavor, way less oil.
  3. The egg in the filling is the glue. Do not skip it.
  4. Let it rest before serving. It firms up as it cools.
  5. Leftovers reheat beautifully the next day.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggplants, sliced lengthwise 1/4 inch thick
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 15 oz ricotta
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella, divided
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan, divided
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • Salt and pepper

How to make it

Directions

  1. Salt the eggplant slices and let them sit 15 minutes. Pat dry, brush with olive oil, and roast at 425F for 12 minutes until pliable.
  2. Mix the ricotta, half the mozzarella, half the parmesan, the egg, garlic, basil, salt, and pepper.
  3. Spread a thin layer of marinara in a baking dish.
  4. Spoon filling onto each eggplant slice, roll up, and place seam side down in the dish.
  5. Top with remaining marinara, mozzarella, and parmesan. Bake 25 minutes until bubbly.
  6. Rest 10 minutes before serving so the rolls hold together.
Money saver. Eggplant is cheapest in late summer. Make a double batch and freeze half the unbaked rolls for later.
