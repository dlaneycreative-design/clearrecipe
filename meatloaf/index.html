Meatloaf

Classic homestyle meatloaf with a sweet tangy glaze, moist and flavorful inside with caramelized edges. Better than you remember.

Prep 15 minsCook 1 hrServes 6
#American#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Meatloaf, in 5 ideas

  1. A free form loaf on a sheet pan gives more caramelized surface than a loaf pan.
  2. Soaking the breadcrumbs in milk (a panade) keeps the meatloaf moist.
  3. The glaze caramelizes in the oven into a sticky sweet crust.
  4. Do not overmix or the meatloaf gets dense. Mix just until combined.
  5. Let it rest so the juices redistribute instead of running out onto the cutting board.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs ground beef (80/20)
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard mixed with 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1/2 cup ketchup
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar

How to make it

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 350F. Line a sheet pan with foil.
  2. Soak the breadcrumbs in the milk for 5 minutes.
  3. Mix the beef, soaked breadcrumbs, eggs, onion, garlic, Worcestershire, Dijon, salt, and pepper gently. Do not overmix.
  4. Shape into a loaf on the sheet pan (not in a loaf pan, so the edges caramelize).
  5. Stir the ketchup, brown sugar, and vinegar. Spread over the loaf.
  6. Bake 1 hour until the internal temp reaches 160F. Rest 10 minutes before slicing.
Leftover meatloaf makes the best sandwiches. Thick cold slices on white bread with mayo is a classic for a reason.
