Taramosalata

Creamy, briny fish roe dip whipped with olive oil and lemon. The star of every Greek meze table.

Prep 15 minsCook 0 minsServes 6
#Greek#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Taramosalata, in 5 ideas

  1. Soak the bread well and squeeze it dry, too much water makes it gluey.
  2. Add the oil in a thin stream like mayonnaise for the creamiest result.
  3. White tarama gives the classic pale pink color.
  4. Chill it at least an hour, the flavor deepens.
  5. Serve with warm pita, raw vegetables, or both.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 100g tarama (fish roe)
  • 2 thick slices white bread, crusts removed
  • 150ml olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 small onion, grated
  • Warm pita, to serve

How to make it

Directions

  1. Soak the bread in water 5 minutes, then squeeze completely dry.
  2. Blend the tarama, bread, onion and lemon juice into a smooth paste.
  3. With the blender running, drizzle in the olive oil in a thin stream until thick and creamy.
  4. Taste and adjust lemon and salt.
  5. Chill at least 1 hour and serve with warm pita.
Money saver. A small tub of tarama stretches into a big bowl of dip, making this the cheapest party food that looks fancy.
