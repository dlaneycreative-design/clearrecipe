California Roll

The inside out sushi roll that started it all: crab, avocado, and cucumber wrapped in seasoned rice and nori. Easier to roll than you think.

Prep 30 minsCook 20 minsServes 4
#Japanese#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

California Roll, in 5 ideas

  1. Wet hands prevent the rice from sticking to everything including you
  2. A sharp wet knife is the secret to clean cuts that do not squash the roll
  3. Do not overfill, restraint makes tighter, prettier rolls
  4. Season the rice while it is hot so the vinegar absorbs evenly
  5. Your first roll will be ugly and delicious, the fifth will look professional

What you need

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sushi rice, cooked
  • 1/4 cup rice vinegar
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 4 sheets nori
  • 8 oz imitation crab sticks
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1/2 cucumber, julienned
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • Soy sauce and wasabi for serving
  • Bamboo sushi mat

How to make it

Directions

  1. Stir the warm rice vinegar, sugar, and salt until dissolved. Fold gently into the hot rice and cool to room temperature.
  2. Lay a nori sheet on the bamboo mat. Spread a thin even layer of rice over it.
  3. Flip the whole sheet over so the rice faces down. Line up crab, avocado, and cucumber along the edge.
  4. Roll tightly using the mat, pressing as you go. Sprinkle the outside with sesame seeds.
  5. Slice with a wet sharp knife into 8 pieces. Serve with soy sauce and wasabi.
Money saver. Imitation crab and a bag of sushi rice make many rolls for the price of one restaurant order.
