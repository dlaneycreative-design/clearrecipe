Tostada de Atun

Sashimi grade tuna on a crispy tostada with avocado, chipotle mayo, and soy. Baja's answer to the poke bowl.

Prep 20 minsCook 0 minsServes 4

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Tostada de Atun, in 5 ideas

  1. This dish comes from Ensenada's mariscos stands
  2. Sashimi grade tuna is essential since it is served raw
  3. The chipotle mayo bridges Mexican and Japanese flavors perfectly
  4. Keep everything ice cold until the moment you assemble
  5. A squeeze of lime brightens the rich tuna and avocado

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 lb sashimi grade tuna, diced
  • 4 tostadas
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp chipotle mayo
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges

How to make it

Directions

  1. Toss the diced tuna with soy sauce and sesame oil.
  2. Spread chipotle mayo on each tostada.
  3. Top with the marinated tuna.
  4. Add avocado, sesame seeds, and green onions.
  5. Serve immediately with lime wedges.
Money saver. Ask the fish counter for the tuna ends or trim pieces; they are perfect for dicing and cost much less than center cut steaks.
