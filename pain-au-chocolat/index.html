Pain au Chocolat

Flaky laminated pastry wrapped around batons of dark chocolate. The bakery classic worth every fold.

Prep 1 hr plus overnight restCook 20 minsServes 8
#French#Bakings

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Pain au Chocolat, in 5 ideas

  1. Keep the pastry cold between steps so the butter layers stay distinct.
  2. Two batons of chocolate per pastry gives the classic double stripe.
  3. Proofing before baking gives a lighter, airier crumb.
  4. Brush the egg wash gently so you do not deflate the layers.
  5. Eat them warm, within hours of baking.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 lb frozen puff pastry, thawed (or homemade croissant dough)
  • 8 oz dark chocolate batons or chopped chocolate
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp milk
  • Flour for dusting
  • Coarse sugar for sprinkling (optional)

How to make it

Directions

  1. Roll the puff pastry into a rectangle about 1/4 inch thick on a floured surface.
  2. Cut into 8 rectangles, roughly 4 by 6 inches each.
  3. Place a chocolate baton near one short edge of each rectangle.
  4. Roll the pastry over the chocolate, add a second baton, and continue rolling.
  5. Place seam side down on a parchment lined baking sheet.
  6. Brush with the egg beaten with milk.
  7. Let proof at room temperature 1 hour until slightly puffy.
  8. Bake at 400 F for 18 to 20 minutes until deep golden and crisp.
Good quality all butter puff pastry from the freezer aisle saves a full day of laminating.
