Austrian · Dessert · Medium

Kaiserschmarrn

The Emperor's shredded pancake: fluffy torn pieces caramelized in butter with rum-soaked raisins, dusted with powdered sugar.

Prep 15 minsCook 15 minsServes 4
#Austrian#Desserts

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Kaiserschmarrn, in 5 ideas

  1. The torn, rustic pieces are the whole point; do not try to make them neat
  2. Rum-soaked raisins are traditional, but apple chunks work too
  3. A hot oven finish gives the signature caramelized edges
  4. Serve it as dessert or as a sweet lunch, both are authentic
  5. Plum compote is the classic partner

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup raisins
  • 3 tablespoons rum
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 4 eggs, separated
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons powdered sugar, plus more for dusting
  • Plum compote or applesauce for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Soak the raisins in the rum for at least 15 minutes.
  2. Whisk the flour, milk, egg yolks, granulated sugar, vanilla, and salt into a smooth batter. Stir in the raisins with their rum.
  3. Beat the egg whites to soft peaks and fold into the batter.
  4. Melt the butter in a large oven-safe skillet over medium heat. Pour in the batter and cook until the bottom is golden, about 4 minutes.
  5. Tear the pancake into rough pieces with two forks, sprinkle with powdered sugar, and transfer the skillet to a 400 F oven for 8 minutes to caramelize.
  6. Dust generously with powdered sugar and serve warm with plum compote.
Money saver. Rum extract mixed with water works if you do not keep rum on hand; a little goes a long way.
