Mille-Feuille

A thousand layers of shattering puff pastry with vanilla pastry cream. The Napoleon of French patisserie.

Prep 45 mins plus chillingCook 25 minsServes 8
#French#Desserts

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Mille-Feuille, in 5 ideas

  1. Baking between pans keeps the pastry flat and extra crisp.
  2. Dock the dough well or it puffs into a pillow.
  3. Chill the pastry cream fully or it will melt the layers.
  4. Use a serrated knife with a gentle sawing motion to slice cleanly.
  5. Assemble within a few hours of serving so the pastry stays crisp.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 lb puff pastry
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 vanilla bean, split, or 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Powdered sugar for dusting
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream, whipped (optional)

How to make it

Directions

  1. Roll the puff pastry thin and dock all over with a fork.
  2. Bake between two sheet pans at 400 F for 20 to 25 minutes until deep golden.
  3. Cool completely, then trim into 3 equal rectangles.
  4. Heat the milk with the vanilla bean until steaming.
  5. Whisk the yolks, sugar, and cornstarch, then temper with the hot milk.
  6. Return to the heat and whisk until thick, then stir in the butter.
  7. Chill the pastry cream with plastic pressed on the surface.
  8. Layer pastry, cream, pastry, cream, pastry, dust with powdered sugar, and slice with a serrated knife.
Store bought puff pastry makes this an achievable weekend project instead of a two day marathon.
