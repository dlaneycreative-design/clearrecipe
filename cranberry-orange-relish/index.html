Cranberry Orange Relish

A raw, bright relish of cranberries, whole orange, and a little sugar, chopped fine and served cold. It cuts through all the rich food like sunshine.

Prep 10 mins plus chillingCook 0 minsServes 8 to 10
#American#Sides

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Cranberry Orange Relish, in 5 ideas

  1. The whole orange, peel and all, gives the relish its perfume and gentle bitterness.
  2. Pulse, never puree. The relish should have texture, like a fine confetti.
  3. Time is an ingredient here. Freshly made it is harsh, by tomorrow it is harmonious.
  4. It is raw, so it brings a fresh, cold contrast nothing else on the table offers.
  5. Leftovers are gold on turkey sandwiches with a swipe of mayonnaise.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 12 oz fresh cranberries
  • 1 large navel orange, unpeeled and quartered
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup chopped walnuts (optional)
  • 1 tbsp orange liqueur or orange juice
  • Pinch of salt

How to make it

Directions

  1. Rinse the cranberries and pick out any soft ones. Cut the orange into quarters and remove any seeds, leaving the peel on.
  2. Pulse the cranberries and orange quarters in a food processor in short bursts until finely chopped but not pureed. Work in two batches if needed.
  3. Scrape into a bowl and stir in the sugar, walnuts if using, the liqueur or juice, and the salt.
  4. Cover and refrigerate at least 4 hours, ideally overnight, so the sugar dissolves and the sharp edges mellow.
  5. Stir once more, taste for sweetness, and serve cold in a small glass bowl.
Make this on Tuesday. It keeps a full week in the fridge and genuinely improves every day, which makes it the easiest win on the whole menu.
