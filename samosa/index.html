Samosa

Crisp golden pastry triangles stuffed with spiced potatoes and peas, the king of Indian snacks.

Prep 40 minsCook 30 minsServes 8
#Indian#Starters

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Samosa, in 5 ideas

  1. The dough must be firm, not soft, for a crisp shell.
  2. Cool filling prevents soggy pastry.
  3. Fry at a steady 350F; too hot browns the outside before the inside cooks.
  4. Seal edges well or they will burst open in the oil.
  5. Freeze uncooked samosas and fry straight from frozen for easy entertaining.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 cup oil, plus more for frying
  • 1/2 cup cold water
  • 4 potatoes, boiled and diced
  • 1 cup peas
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tbsp garam masala and 1 tsp amchur
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup chopped cilantro
  • Oil for deep frying

How to make it

Directions

  1. Rub oil into flour and salt, add water, and knead to a firm dough. Rest 30 minutes.
  2. Saute cumin seeds and onion until soft; add peas, potatoes, garam masala, amchur, and salt.
  3. Cool the filling completely and stir in cilantro.
  4. Roll dough into ovals, cut in half, and form cones, sealing edges with water.
  5. Fill each cone, seal the top, and crimp.
  6. Deep fry in 350F oil until deep golden, about 6 minutes per batch.
  7. Drain on a rack and serve with tamarind chutney.
Money saver. Homemade samosas cost a fraction of takeout and freeze beautifully.
