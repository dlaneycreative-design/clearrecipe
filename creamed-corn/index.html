Creamed Corn

Sweet corn simmered in a lightly thickened cream with butter and a hint of nutmeg. Rich, simple, and deeply comforting.

Prep 10 minsCook 20 minsServes 8
#American#Sides

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Creamed Corn, in 5 ideas

  1. Blending part of the corn thickens the dish naturally, no heavy starch needed.
  2. A whisper of nutmeg makes cream based dishes taste warmer and richer.
  3. Keep the simmer gentle. A hard boil can split the cream and dull the color.
  4. Frozen corn is excellent here and saves serious prep time on a busy day.
  5. A spoonful of sugar only if your corn needs it. Taste before you add.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 6 cups corn kernels (fresh, or frozen and thawed)
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup whole milk
  • 1 tbsp all purpose flour
  • 1 tsp granulated sugar
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • Pinch of ground nutmeg
  • 2 tbsp chopped chives

How to make it

Directions

  1. Blend 2 cups of the corn with the milk until roughly pureed. This becomes the thickener and keeps the flavor all corn.
  2. Melt the butter in a large saucepan and soften the onion over medium heat for 4 minutes without browning.
  3. Sprinkle in the flour and stir for 1 minute.
  4. Add the remaining whole kernels, the corn puree, the cream, sugar, salt, pepper, and nutmeg.
  5. Simmer gently for 10 to 12 minutes, stirring often, until glossy and thick enough to mound on a spoon.
  6. Taste for seasoning, scatter with chives, and serve warm in a shallow bowl.
If you use fresh corn, simmer the stripped cobs in the cream for 10 minutes first, then discard them. The cobs give up a surprising amount of sweet corn flavor.
