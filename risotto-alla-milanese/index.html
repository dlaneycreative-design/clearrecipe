Risotto alla Milanese

Creamy saffron risotto with Parmesan, golden and luxurious. The classic partner to osso buco.

Prep 10 minsCook 30 minsServes 4
#Italian#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Risotto alla Milanese, in 5 ideas

  1. Keep the broth warm or the rice stops cooking and turns gummy.
  2. Stir often but not constantly, the rice needs time to release starch.
  3. All onda means the risotto should ripple when you shake the pan.
  4. Taste for doneness starting at 18 minutes, it goes fast.
  5. Serve immediately, risotto waits for no one.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 6 cups chicken broth, kept warm
  • 1/2 tsp saffron threads
  • 3 tbsp butter, divided
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 1 1/2 cups arborio rice
  • 1/2 cup dry white wine
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

How to make it

Directions

  1. Steep the saffron in 1/2 cup of the warm broth for 10 minutes.
  2. Heat 2 tbsp butter and the olive oil in a wide pan over medium heat.
  3. Cook the onion 4 minutes until soft and translucent.
  4. Add the rice and toast 2 minutes, stirring, until the grains are pearly.
  5. Pour in the wine and stir until absorbed.
  6. Add the warm broth one ladle at a time, stirring often and letting each addition absorb before adding more.
  7. Stir in the saffron broth about halfway through for even golden color.
  8. After 18 to 20 minutes the rice should be creamy and al dente. Remove from heat, beat in the remaining butter and Parmesan, and season.
