Mexican · Starter · Easy

Queso Fundido

Mexico's bubbling cheese: Oaxaca cheese melted with chorizo and poblano, scooped up with warm tortillas straight from the skillet.

Prep 10 minsCook 15 minsServes 6
#Mexican#Starters

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Queso Fundido, in 5 ideas

  1. Oaxaca cheese gives the authentic stretchy pull; mozzarella is the best substitute
  2. Serve it screaming hot; it firms up as it cools
  3. Roast the poblano directly over a gas flame for easy peeling
  4. Warm the tortillas while the cheese broils
  5. A cast iron skillet keeps it bubbling at the table

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Oaxaca or mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • 1/2 lb Mexican chorizo, casings removed
  • 1 poblano pepper, roasted, peeled, and diced
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
  • Warm flour tortillas for serving
  • Lime wedges

How to make it

Directions

  1. Brown the chorizo in an oven-safe skillet over medium heat, breaking it up, about 6 minutes. Remove with a slotted spoon.
  2. In the same skillet, soften the onion, garlic, and poblano in the chorizo fat for 4 minutes.
  3. Return the chorizo to the skillet and spread evenly.
  4. Top with the shredded cheese and broil 3 to 5 minutes until bubbling and lightly browned.
  5. Shower with cilantro and serve immediately in the skillet.
  6. Scoop onto warm tortillas with a squeeze of lime.
Money saver. Mexican chorizo is sold inexpensively at Latin markets; a little goes a long way in this dish.
