Tortilla Soup

Smoky tomato broth with tender chicken, topped with crispy tortilla strips, avocado, and lime. Comfort in a bowl.

Prep 15 minsCook 30 minsServes 6
#Mexican#Soups

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Tortilla Soup, in 5 ideas

  1. Fry the tortilla strips fresh. They are the soul of the soup.
  2. Blending half gives body while keeping some texture.
  3. Use rotisserie chicken to make this a 30 minute meal.
  4. Add the toppings at the table so everything stays crisp.
  5. A squeeze of lime at the end wakes up all the flavors.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 6 corn tortillas, cut in thin strips
  • 1/3 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced, plus 1 minced jalapeno
  • 1 can (14 oz) fire roasted tomatoes
  • 6 cups chicken broth
  • 1 tsp each cumin and dried oregano
  • 2 cups shredded cooked chicken
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 1/2 cup crumbled cotija cheese
  • Cilantro, lime wedges, for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Fry the tortilla strips in hot oil until crisp and golden, about 3 minutes. Drain on paper towels and salt.
  2. In the same pot, cook the onion until soft, about 6 minutes. Add the garlic and jalapeno for 1 minute.
  3. Add the tomatoes, broth, cumin, and oregano. Simmer 20 minutes.
  4. Blend half the soup until smooth, then return it to the pot for a slightly thickened broth.
  5. Stir in the shredded chicken and salt. Heat through 5 minutes.
  6. Ladle into bowls and top with tortilla strips, avocado, cotija, cilantro, and lime.
Save the chicken carcass from a roast chicken to make the broth. Free and better than store bought.
