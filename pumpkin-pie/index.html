Pumpkin Pie

Silky spiced pumpkin custard in a flaky crust, crowned with softly whipped cream. The one dessert Thanksgiving cannot happen without.

Prep 20 minsCook 55 minsServes 8
#American#Desserts

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Pumpkin Pie, in 5 ideas

  1. The hot start sets the crust and the gentle finish keeps the custard crack free.
  2. A slight jiggle in the center is the goal. A fully firm pie in the oven becomes an overbaked pie on the plate.
  3. Brown sugar gives a deeper, rounder sweetness than white sugar.
  4. Chilling the crust before filling helps it bake up flaky instead of shrinking.
  5. Pumpkin pie tastes better the next day, so baking it Wednesday is strategy, not laziness.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 unbaked 9 inch pie crust
  • 1 can (15 oz) pumpkin puree
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1/4 tsp ground cloves
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 large eggs plus 1 yolk
  • 1 can (12 oz) evaporated milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Whipped cream, for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Fit the crust into a 9 inch pie plate, crimp the edges, and chill it while you make the filling.
  2. Whisk the pumpkin, brown sugar, spices, and salt in a large bowl until smooth.
  3. Whisk in the eggs and yolk, then slowly whisk in the evaporated milk and vanilla until the filling is silky and uniform.
  4. Pour the filling into the chilled crust and smooth the top.
  5. Bake at 425°F for 15 minutes, then reduce the heat to 350°F and bake 40 to 45 minutes more, until the edges are set and the center jiggles slightly.
  6. Cool on a rack for at least 2 hours. The custard finishes setting as it cools.
  7. Serve at room temperature or chilled, each slice with a generous cloud of whipped cream.
If the crust edges brown too fast, cover them with a ring of foil for the last 20 minutes. And always check the can twice: pumpkin puree, not pumpkin pie filling, or the spices double up.
