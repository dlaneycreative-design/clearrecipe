Cornbread Stuffing

Crumbly golden cornbread tossed with sweet onion, celery, and herbs in a rich stock. A Southern leaning stuffing with real character.

Prep 25 minsCook 40 minsServes 10
#American#Sides

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Cornbread Stuffing, in 5 ideas

  1. Bake the cornbread a day or two early. Slightly stale cornbread makes far better stuffing.
  2. A mix of fine crumbs and bigger chunks gives the best texture, tender with a little bite.
  3. Bell pepper adds a gentle sweetness that plays well with the corn.
  4. Cornbread is thirsty, but resist drowning it. It should be moist, never wet.
  5. This stuffing loves a drizzle of turkey gravy more than any other side on the table.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 8 cups crumbled day old cornbread
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 3 celery stalks, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 tsp rubbed sage
  • 2 cups turkey or chicken stock
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt

How to make it

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Spread the crumbled cornbread on a baking sheet and toast 10 minutes to dry it out.
  2. Melt the butter in a large skillet. Cook the onion, celery, and bell pepper for 8 minutes until soft.
  3. Add the garlic, thyme, and sage and cook 1 minute more. Remove from the heat.
  4. Combine the toasted cornbread and vegetables in a large bowl and toss gently so the cornbread keeps some texture.
  5. Whisk the eggs into the stock and pour over the mixture in two additions, tossing between each, until moistened throughout.
  6. Season with salt and pepper and spread into a buttered 9x13 inch dish, pressing lightly.
  7. Bake uncovered for 35 to 40 minutes until the top is crisp and golden and the center is set. Rest 10 minutes before serving.
Use a cast iron baked cornbread with crisp edges if you can. Those browned edges crumbled through the stuffing add little pockets of toasty flavor.
