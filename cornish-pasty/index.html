Cornish Pasty

Cornwall's hand held pie: beef, potato and swede in flaky pastry with a crimped edge. A whole dinner you can hold.

Prep 30 minsCook 45 minsServes 4
#British#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Cornish Pasty, in 5 ideas

  1. The crimp is the signature, practice makes perfect.
  2. No carrots, Cornish law says only swede (rutabaga).
  3. Season boldly, the filling needs more pepper than you think.
  4. Cut a steam hole so the pastry stays crisp.
  5. They freeze brilliantly, baked or unbaked.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 500g shortcrust pastry
  • 300g beef skirt, diced small
  • 300g potatoes, diced small
  • 200g swede, diced small
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Salt and plenty of black pepper
  • 1 egg, beaten

How to make it

Directions

  1. Mix the raw beef, potato, swede and onion with salt and a generous amount of pepper.
  2. Roll the pastry into 4 circles about 20cm across.
  3. Pile the filling on one half of each circle, dot with butter.
  4. Fold over, seal and crimp the edge firmly.
  5. Brush with beaten egg, cut a small steam hole, and bake at 180C (350F) for 40 to 45 minutes until golden.
Money saver. Beef skirt is a cheap cut that becomes meltingly tender inside the pastry.
