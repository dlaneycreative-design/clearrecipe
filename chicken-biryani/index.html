Chicken Biryani

Fragrant basmati rice layered with spiced chicken, saffron, and crispy onions in one glorious pot.

Prep 30 mins plus marinatingCook 1 hrServes 6
#Indian#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Chicken Biryani, in 5 ideas

  1. Bone-in chicken stays moist during the long steam and adds flavor to the rice.
  2. Do not skip frying the onions; they add sweetness and crunch that define biryani.
  3. A tight lid is essential; seal the edge with dough or a kitchen towel under the lid.
  4. Let it rest 10 minutes before opening so the steam finishes the rice.
  5. Serve with raita to cool the spices.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs chicken thighs, bone-in
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp biryani masala
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp salt, divided
  • 3 cups basmati rice
  • 1/2 tsp saffron threads soaked in 1/4 cup warm milk
  • 3 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 4 cups water
  • 1/2 cup chopped cilantro and mint
  • 4 cardamom pods and 2 bay leaves

How to make it

Directions

  1. Marinate chicken in yogurt, biryani masala, turmeric, and 1 tsp salt for at least 1 hour.
  2. Soak saffron in warm milk and set aside.
  3. Fry onions in oil until deep golden and crisp; drain on paper towels.
  4. Parboil rice with cardamom, bay leaves, and remaining salt until 70 percent cooked; drain.
  5. Layer half the rice in a heavy pot, then the chicken with its marinade, then remaining rice.
  6. Drizzle saffron milk over the top and scatter with fried onions, cilantro, and mint.
  7. Cover tightly and cook on low for 40 minutes until the chicken is tender and rice is fluffy.
Money saver. Buy biryani masala in bulk at an Indian grocery; it costs a fraction of the supermarket jar.
