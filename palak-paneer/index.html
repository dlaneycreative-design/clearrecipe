Palak Paneer

Creamy spinach curry studded with golden cubes of paneer cheese, a vegetarian classic done right.

Prep 15 minsCook 25 minsServes 4
#Indian#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Palak Paneer, in 5 ideas

  1. Blanching keeps the spinach vividly green instead of dull.
  2. Searing the paneer first keeps it from falling apart in the curry.
  3. A squeeze of lemon at the end brightens the whole dish.
  4. If the curry is bitter, a pinch of sugar balances it.
  5. Serve with hot naan for scooping.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 lb fresh spinach
  • 12 oz paneer, cubed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, 1 inch ginger, and 2 green chilies
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp salt

How to make it

Directions

  1. Blanch spinach in boiling water for 2 minutes, then shock in ice water and blend smooth.
  2. Sear paneer cubes in oil until golden on all sides; set aside.
  3. In the same pan, bloom cumin seeds, then saute onion, garlic, ginger, and chilies until soft.
  4. Add tomatoes, turmeric, and salt; cook until the tomatoes break down.
  5. Stir in the spinach puree and simmer 10 minutes.
  6. Add cream and garam masala, then fold in the paneer and warm through.
Money saver. Make your own paneer from a gallon of milk; it costs less than store bought and tastes better.
