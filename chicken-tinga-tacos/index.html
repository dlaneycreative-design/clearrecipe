Chicken Tinga Tacos

Smoky shredded chicken simmered in tomato chipotle sauce, piled into warm corn tortillas. Weeknight taco royalty.

Prep 15 minsCook 25 minsServes 4
#Mexican#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Chicken Tinga Tacos, in 5 ideas

  1. Chicken thighs stay juicy when shredded. Breasts dry out fast in this sauce.
  2. Char the tortillas. A little blackening adds real taco truck flavor.
  3. Control the heat with the chipotles. Two is medium, one is mild, three is brave.
  4. The tinga keeps 4 days in the fridge and the flavor deepens overnight.
  5. Double the batch and freeze half. Future you will be grateful.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 onion, half sliced and half diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 can (14 oz) fire roasted tomatoes
  • 2 chipotle peppers in adobo, plus 1 tbsp sauce
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • Diced onion, cilantro, and lime wedges, for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Simmer the chicken thighs in the broth with the halved onion and 2 garlic cloves for 15 minutes, until cooked through. Shred the meat and save the broth.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, chipotles with adobo sauce, oregano, cumin, and the remaining garlic until smooth.
  3. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium high. Cook the diced onion until soft, then pour in the blended sauce.
  4. Simmer the sauce 5 minutes until it darkens slightly. Add the shredded chicken and 1/2 cup of the reserved broth.
  5. Cook 10 minutes, stirring, until the chicken soaks up the sauce. Season with salt.
  6. Warm the tortillas directly over a gas flame or in a dry skillet. Fill with tinga and top with onion, cilantro, and lime.
Buy a big can of chipotles in adobo and freeze the leftovers in ice cube trays. You will use them constantly.
