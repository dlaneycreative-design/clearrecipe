Corned Beef Hash

Crispy diced potatoes and savory corned beef with onions and peppers, topped with a fried egg. The best use of leftover corned beef.

Prep 10 minsCook 20 minsServes 4
#American#Breakfasts

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Corned Beef Hash, in 5 ideas

  1. A cast iron skillet gives the best crust on the potatoes.
  2. Par boiling the potatoes first guarantees they cook through and crisp up.
  3. Press the hash down and leave it alone. Constant stirring prevents browning.
  4. The crispy bits are the best part. Let them develop.
  5. A runny yolk on top acts as a sauce for the whole dish.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 3 cups diced cooked corned beef
  • 3 cups diced potatoes (1/2 inch)
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 4 eggs
  • Hot sauce for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Boil the diced potatoes 5 minutes until just tender. Drain well.
  2. Heat butter and oil in a large cast iron skillet over medium high heat.
  3. Add the potatoes in a single layer. Cook undisturbed 5 minutes until crispy on the bottom.
  4. Add the onion and bell pepper, cook 3 minutes.
  5. Stir in the corned beef and Worcestershire. Press down and cook 5 more minutes until crispy in spots.
  6. Make 4 wells and crack an egg into each. Cover and cook 3 minutes until the whites set.
  7. Season with salt and pepper. Serve with hot sauce.
This is the reason to make extra corned beef on St. Patrick's Day. The leftovers are better than the original dinner.
