Diavola Pizza

Blistered crust, spicy salami, and a drizzle of hot honey. Sweet heat on the perfect chewy base.

Prep 20 mins plus dough restingCook 10 minsServes 2
#Italian#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Diavola Pizza, in 5 ideas

  1. A screaming hot oven is everything. Give the stone a full 45 minutes to heat.
  2. Less sauce is more. Too much makes the center soggy.
  3. Tear the mozzarella and let it drain a bit so it does not waterlog the pizza.
  4. Hot honey goes on after baking so it stays glossy and bright.
  5. Let the pizza rest 2 minutes before slicing so the cheese sets.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 ball pizza dough (store bought or homemade)
  • 1/2 cup crushed San Marzano tomatoes
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tsp olive oil, plus more for drizzling
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 6 oz fresh mozzarella, torn
  • 3 oz spicy salami, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 2 tbsp hot honey
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Semolina or flour, for dusting

How to make it

Directions

  1. Place a pizza stone or steel in the oven and heat to the hottest setting, 500 to 550 F, for at least 45 minutes.
  2. Stir the tomatoes, garlic, olive oil, and salt into a quick sauce. No cooking needed.
  3. Stretch the dough on a floured peel to a 12 inch round.
  4. Spread a thin layer of sauce, leaving a 1 inch border. Scatter the mozzarella and salami.
  5. Launch onto the hot stone. Bake 7 to 10 minutes until the crust is puffed and charred in spots.
  6. Finish with red pepper flakes, a drizzle of hot honey, basil, and olive oil.
Store bought dough from the deli section works great and costs a couple of dollars. No shame in it.
