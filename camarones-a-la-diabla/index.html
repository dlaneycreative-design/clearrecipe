Camarones a la Diabla

Deviled shrimp in a fiery red chile sauce. Bold, spicy, and dangerously easy to keep eating.

Prep 15 minsCook 15 minsServes 4
#American#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Camarones a la Diabla, in 5 ideas

  1. A la diabla means devil style; the arbol chiles bring the real heat
  2. Straining the sauce gives it a silky restaurant quality texture
  3. Shrimp cook in minutes, so have everything ready before they hit the pan
  4. The sauce clings best when it is reduced enough to coat a spoon
  5. Rice is essential to soak up every drop of the spicy sauce

What you need

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 4 dried guajillo chiles, stemmed and seeded
  • 2 dried arbol chiles
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 onion
  • 1 cup shrimp or chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Lime wedges and rice, for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Soak the chiles in hot water 15 minutes, then blend with garlic, onion, and stock into a smooth sauce.
  2. Strain the sauce into a skillet and simmer 8 minutes until slightly thickened.
  3. Season the shrimp with salt.
  4. Sear the shrimp in butter and oil 2 minutes per side.
  5. Pour the diabla sauce over the shrimp and simmer 3 minutes.
  6. Serve with lime wedges over rice.
Money saver. Buy frozen shrimp in the bag; it is often fresher than the thawed shrimp at the counter and much cheaper.
