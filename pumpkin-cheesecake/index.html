Pumpkin Cheesecake

All the warm spice of pumpkin pie in a creamy, elegant cheesecake with a gingersnap crust. It slices like a dream and might just upstage the pie this year.

Prep 25 minsCook 55 minsServes 12
#American#Desserts

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Pumpkin Cheesecake, in 5 ideas

  1. Swirl a few spoonfuls of batter without pumpkin on top for a marbled finish.
  2. A water bath and a slow cool keep the top smooth and crack free.
  3. Make it two days ahead. Cheesecake genuinely tastes better after a rest.
  4. Candied pecans scattered on top echo the pecan pie sitting next to it.
  5. Serve small slices. It is rich, and seconds are always available.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups gingersnap crumbs
  • 5 tbsp butter, melted
  • 3 packages (8 oz each) cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 can (15 oz) pumpkin puree
  • 3 eggs, room temperature
  • 1/4 cup sour cream
  • 1 1/2 tsp pumpkin pie spice
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Whipped cream, for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 325°F. Mix the gingersnap crumbs with the melted butter and press firmly into the bottom of a 9 inch springform pan.
  2. Beat the cream cheese and sugar until completely smooth, scraping the bowl well.
  3. Beat in the pumpkin puree, sour cream, pumpkin pie spice, and vanilla.
  4. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing on low just until each disappears. Do not overmix.
  5. Pour the batter over the crust. Wrap the pan in foil and set it in a larger pan with an inch of hot water.
  6. Bake 55 to 65 minutes, until the edges are set and the center still has a gentle wobble.
  7. Turn the oven off, crack the door, and let the cheesecake cool inside for 1 hour. Then chill at least 4 hours or overnight before slicing.
Room temperature cream cheese and eggs are nonnegotiable. Cold ingredients are the number one cause of lumpy batter and cracked tops.
