Congee

Silky rice porridge simmered low and slow with ginger and chicken stock. China's ultimate comfort bowl, endlessly customizable with toppings.

Prep 5 minsCook 1 hr 15 minsServes 4
#Chinese#Breakfasts

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Congee, in 5 ideas

  1. Low and slow is non-negotiable. A hard boil makes it gluey. The barest simmer makes it silky.
  2. Stock beats water. Water works, but chicken stock gives the porridge a savory depth from the start.
  3. Toppings go on at the table. Never in the pot. Everyone likes a different bowl.
  4. Leftover rice works too. Use 3 cups cooked rice and 6 cups stock, simmer 30 minutes. Faster, still silky.
  5. White pepper, not black. It is the classic seasoning here, with a gentle heat that suits the mild porridge.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 cup jasmine rice
  • 8 cups chicken stock (or water)
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/2 tsp ground white pepper
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • Sliced scallions, for topping
  • Shredded cooked chicken, for topping
  • Soft-boiled eggs, for topping
  • Crispy fried shallots, for topping
  • Soy sauce, for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Rinse the rice under cold water until the water runs mostly clear. Drain.
  2. Bring the stock to a boil in a large heavy pot. Add the rice and ginger.
  3. Reduce to the lowest simmer. Cook 60 to 75 minutes, stirring every 10 minutes, until the grains break down and the porridge turns creamy.
  4. Season with the salt and white pepper. Taste and adjust.
  5. Stir in the sesame oil.
  6. Ladle into bowls. Set out the toppings and let everyone build their own bowl with a splash of soy sauce.
Stir more than you think. Every 10 minutes at least. The rice sticks, and the creaminess comes from the grains breaking down against the pot.
