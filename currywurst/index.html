Currywurst

Grilled bratwurst sliced and drowned in curry ketchup with fries. Berlin street food royalty.

Prep 10 minsCook 20 minsServes 4
#German#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Currywurst, in 5 ideas

  1. The sauce should be tangy and warmly spiced, not just ketchup with curry.
  2. Steam the sausage first, then crisp it on the grill.
  3. Dust extra curry powder on top at the end.
  4. Skin on fries are the traditional partner.
  5. It is street food, eat it standing up with a fork.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 4 bratwurst
  • 200ml ketchup
  • 2 tbsp curry powder, plus more to dust
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Fries, to serve

How to make it

Directions

  1. Simmer the ketchup, curry powder, Worcestershire, paprika and onion 10 minutes into a sauce.
  2. Grill or pan fry the bratwurst until browned and cooked through.
  3. Slice the sausages and arrange over fries.
  4. Ladle over the warm curry ketchup.
  5. Dust with more curry powder and serve immediately.
Money saver. Bratwurst is often cheapest in bulk at German or Eastern European delis.
