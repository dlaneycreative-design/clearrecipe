Italian Wedding Soup

Tiny pork meatballs and acini di pepe pasta in a rich chicken broth with fresh spinach. Cozy comfort in every spoonful.

Prep 25 minsCook 30 minsServes 6
#Italian#Soups

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Italian Wedding Soup, in 5 ideas

  1. Keep the meatballs small so you get one in every spoonful.
  2. Do not overmix the meat or the meatballs turn dense.
  3. Add the spinach at the very end so it stays bright green.
  4. Acini di pepe is traditional, but orzo or ditalini work fine.
  5. Leftovers keep 3 days in the fridge and reheat beautifully.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1/2 lb ground pork
  • 1/2 lb ground beef
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan, plus more for serving
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 8 cups chicken broth
  • 3/4 cup acini di pepe or orzo
  • 4 cups fresh baby spinach
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced

How to make it

Directions

  1. Mix the pork, beef, breadcrumbs, Parmesan, egg, garlic, and Italian seasoning until just combined.
  2. Roll into small meatballs about 3/4 inch across and set on a baking sheet.
  3. Simmer the chicken broth in a large pot, then add the carrot and celery.
  4. Gently drop in the meatballs and simmer 10 minutes until cooked through.
  5. Stir in the acini di pepe and cook 8 minutes until al dente.
  6. Add the spinach and stir until wilted, about 2 minutes.
  7. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  8. Serve hot with extra grated Parmesan.
Make double the meatballs and freeze half raw on a tray, then bag them for a future batch.
