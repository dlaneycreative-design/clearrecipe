Bubble and Squeak

Leftover potato and cabbage fried into a crisp golden cake. The best thing to happen to leftovers.

Prep 10 minsCook 20 minsServes 4

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Bubble and Squeak, in 5 ideas

  1. Cold leftovers work best, they hold together.
  2. A cast iron pan gives the crispiest crust.
  3. Press it down and leave it alone, patience makes the crust.
  4. Bacon bits take it from good to great.
  5. A fried egg on top makes it dinner.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 500g leftover mashed potato (cold)
  • 300g cooked cabbage, chopped
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 4 rashers bacon, chopped (optional)
  • Salt and pepper

How to make it

Directions

  1. Fry the bacon and onion in butter until the onion softens.
  2. Add the cabbage and cook 3 minutes.
  3. Mix in the cold mashed potato and season well.
  4. Press into a thick cake in the pan and cook undisturbed 8 minutes until deeply golden underneath.
  5. Flip in sections (or slide under a grill) and crisp the other side. Serve hot.
Money saver. This exists to use leftovers, which makes it essentially free.
