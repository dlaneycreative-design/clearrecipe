Baked Ziti

Ziti layered with sausage-spiked marinara, creamy ricotta, and a golden blanket of melted mozzarella, baked until bubbly and browned at the edges.

Prep 20 minsCook 35 minsServes 8
#Italian#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Baked Ziti, in 5 ideas

  1. Undercook the pasta by 2 minutes, it finishes in the oven, and fully cooked pasta turns to mush.
  2. The egg in the ricotta binds it into creamy layers instead of a grainy smear.
  3. Rest the dish before cutting, slicing straight out of the oven gives you a sloppy pile instead of clean layers.
  4. Shred your own mozzarella, low-moisture block cheese melts smoothly while pre-shredded bags stay grainy.
  5. Browning the sausage well before the sauce goes in builds the deep flavor that carries the whole dish.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ziti
  • 1 lb Italian sausage, casings removed
  • 1 28-oz can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 15-oz container ricotta cheese
  • 1 lb low-moisture mozzarella, shredded
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 egg
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh basil, for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Boil the ziti until 2 minutes shy of al dente. Drain.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Crumble in the sausage and cook until browned, about 7 minutes. Add the garlic and Italian seasoning and cook 1 minute.
  3. Pour in the crushed tomatoes, season with salt and pepper, and simmer 10 minutes.
  4. In a bowl, stir the ricotta with the egg, half the Parmesan, salt, and pepper until smooth.
  5. In a 9x13 baking dish, layer: half the ziti, half the ricotta mixture, half the sauce, half the mozzarella. Repeat the layers once more, ending with mozzarella and the remaining Parmesan.
  6. Cover with foil and bake 20 minutes. Uncover and bake 15 more minutes until golden and bubbling.
  7. Rest 10 minutes before serving so the layers set. Top with fresh basil.
Baked ziti is a freezer hero, assemble it in a foil pan, freeze before baking, and bake from frozen with 20 extra minutes for a no-effort weeknight dinner.
