    Halloween Foods and Candies

    Ten treats from the collection for the spookiest night of the year. Every recipe below is one tap away, ingredients first, and most carry step by step photos so the making is as clear as the eating.

    Halloween cooking splits two ways: the candy bowl and the party table. Make the candies ahead, bake the day of, and keep a pot of mulled cider warm for whoever is handing out treats at the door.

    The candies

    Two homemade candies that beat anything in a wrapper. Buckeyes are Ohio tradition, peanut butter dipped in chocolate with the little circle left bare. Brigadeiros are Brazil’s beloved chocolate truffles, rolled in sprinkles and gone in an hour.

    BuckeyesSmooth peanut butter centers dipped in rich chocolate, left with a little swirl of tan showing on top like the namesake Ohio tree nut. No baking required, just mixing, rolling, and dipping.See the recipe BrigadeiroBrazil's beloved chocolate truffles: sweetened condensed milk and cocoa rolled into fudgy balls and coated in sprinkles. One pan, five ingredients, impossible to stop eating.See the recipe
