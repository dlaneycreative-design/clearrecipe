Kimchi Fried Rice

Tangy funky kimchi fried with day old rice, finished with a fried egg and sesame. Spicy, savory, and deeply satisfying.

Prep 10 minsCook 10 minsServes 4
#Korean#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Kimchi Fried Rice, in 5 ideas

  1. Well fermented sour kimchi makes the best fried rice, fresh kimchi is too mild
  2. The kimchi juice is liquid gold, do not leave it in the jar
  3. Let the rice sit undisturbed for a minute to develop crispy bits
  4. A runny yolk mixed through the rice is the perfect sauce
  5. Spam is the classic pairing and it is delicious, no apologies needed

What you need

Ingredients

  • 4 cups day old cooked rice
  • 2 cups chopped kimchi, plus 2 tbsp kimchi juice
  • 1/2 lb diced spam, bacon, or pork belly (optional)
  • 3 green onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • Nori strips for garnish (optional)

How to make it

Directions

  1. Fry the meat in hot oil 3 minutes until crisp. Add the kimchi and cook 3 minutes until caramelized.
  2. Add the rice, kimchi juice, gochujang, and soy sauce. Fry 4 minutes, breaking up clumps.
  3. Stir in half the green onions and sesame oil.
  4. Fry the eggs sunny side up in a separate pan.
  5. Serve the rice topped with eggs, remaining green onions, sesame seeds, and nori.
Money saver. Kimchi is cheap, lasts for months, and turns leftover rice into a craveable dinner.
