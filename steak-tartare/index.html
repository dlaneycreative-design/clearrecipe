Steak Tartare

Hand chopped raw beef with capers, cornichons, Dijon, and a silky egg yolk. Bold, luxurious, and for the adventurous.

Prep 20 minsCook 0 minsServes 4
#French#Starters

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Steak Tartare, in 5 ideas

  1. Hand chopping gives the perfect texture, grinding makes it mushy.
  2. Keep everything ice cold for food safety and clean flavor.
  3. Use the freshest beef you can buy from a trusted butcher.
  4. Mix gently at the last minute so it stays light.
  5. Very fresh egg yolks only, from a source you trust.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 lb beef tenderloin or top sirloin, very cold
  • 2 tbsp capers, minced
  • 3 cornichons, minced
  • 1 small shallot, minced
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • Toasted baguette for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Trim all fat and sinew from the beef.
  2. Chop the beef by hand into 1/4 inch dice, never use a grinder.
  3. Mix the capers, cornichons, shallot, Dijon, Worcestershire, olive oil, and hot sauce in a bowl.
  4. Fold in the beef gently until just combined.
  5. Season with salt and black pepper.
  6. Divide among chilled plates and shape into rounds.
  7. Make a small well in each and nestle an egg yolk inside.
  8. Sprinkle with parsley and serve with toasted baguette.
Tell your butcher it is for tartare, they will often cut it from the freshest piece.
