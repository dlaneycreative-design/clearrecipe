Shrimp Scampi Pasta

Plump shrimp in a garlicky white wine butter sauce tossed with linguine. Restaurant elegance in 20 minutes flat.

Prep 10 minsCook 15 minsServes 4
#Italian#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Shrimp Scampi Pasta, in 5 ideas

  1. Pat the shrimp very dry. Moisture is the enemy of a good sear.
  2. Do not overcook the garlic. Golden is perfect, brown is bitter.
  3. The starchy pasta water is what makes the sauce cling instead of pool.
  4. Have everything ready before you start. This moves fast.
  5. Serve in warm bowls so the butter sauce does not seize.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 12 oz linguine
  • 1 1/2 lbs large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 5 tbsp butter, divided
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 6 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 cup dry white wine
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1/2 cup pasta water, reserved
  • 1/2 cup parsley, chopped
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Grated parmesan, for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Boil the linguine in well salted water until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water before draining.
  2. Pat the shrimp dry and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat 2 tbsp butter with the olive oil in a large skillet over medium high. Sear the shrimp 2 minutes per side. Remove to a plate.
  4. Lower the heat. Add the remaining butter, garlic, and red pepper flakes. Cook 1 minute until fragrant.
  5. Pour in the wine and lemon juice, scraping up the browned bits. Simmer 2 minutes.
  6. Return the shrimp to the pan. Add the pasta and toss with the sauce, loosening with pasta water as needed.
  7. Shower with parsley and parmesan. Serve immediately.
Frozen shrimp is often fresher than the counter stuff and costs less. Thaw in cold water in 20 minutes.
