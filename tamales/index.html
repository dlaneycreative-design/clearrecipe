Tamales

Fluffy masa steamed in corn husks around savory fillings. A labor of love worth every minute.

Prep 1 hr plus overnight soakingCook 1 hr 30 minsServes 8
#Mexican#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Tamales, in 5 ideas

  1. The float test works. Drop a small ball of masa in water. If it floats, your dough is airy enough.
  2. Do not overfill. Too much filling and they burst in the steamer.
  3. Keep the water at a steady simmer, not a rolling boil, or the husks dry out.
  4. Tamales are a group project. Enlist friends and make a day of it.
  5. They reheat beautifully. Steam 15 minutes from the fridge, 25 from frozen.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 30 dried corn husks
  • 4 cups masa harina
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 1/3 cups lard or vegetable shortening
  • 3 1/2 cups warm chicken broth
  • 2 cups shredded chicken tinga or pork carnitas
  • 1 cup red salsa
  • Sour cream and extra salsa, for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Soak the corn husks in hot water for at least 1 hour until pliable. Drain.
  2. Beat the lard until fluffy, about 3 minutes. Mix in the masa harina, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Add the warm broth gradually, beating until the dough is soft and spreadable. It should hold its shape but spread easily.
  4. Toss the shredded meat with the salsa for the filling.
  5. Spread 1/4 cup masa in the center of a husk, leaving the narrow end clear. Add 2 tbsp filling down the middle.
  6. Fold the sides over, then fold up the narrow end. Place seam side down in a steamer basket.
  7. Steam over simmering water 1 hour 15 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes, until the masa pulls away from the husk cleanly.
Make a huge batch and freeze them in the husks. They keep 3 months and steam straight from frozen.
