Cassoulet

White beans slow baked with duck confit, sausage, and pork under a golden breadcrumb crust. The ultimate French comfort casserole.

Prep 45 mins plus overnight soakingCook 3 hrsServes 8
#French#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Cassoulet, in 5 ideas

  1. Soaking the beans overnight gives them the creamiest texture.
  2. Breaking the crust during baking is the traditional technique.
  3. Use a wide, shallow Dutch oven for the best crust.
  4. Let it rest 20 minutes before serving so it sets up.
  5. Leftovers reheat beautifully and taste even richer.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 lb dried white beans (Great Northern or cannellini), soaked overnight
  • 4 duck confit legs
  • 1 lb garlic sausage or kielbasa, cut in chunks
  • 1/2 lb pork shoulder, cubed
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs tossed with 3 tbsp duck fat or olive oil

How to make it

Directions

  1. Drain the soaked beans and simmer in fresh water 20 minutes until just tender, then drain.
  2. Brown the pork shoulder in a Dutch oven, then remove.
  3. Cook the onion and carrots 6 minutes, add garlic and tomato paste, and cook 2 minutes.
  4. Add the beans, broth, bay leaves, and thyme, and bring to a simmer.
  5. Nestle in the pork, sausage, and duck confit legs.
  6. Simmer covered 1 hour, then uncover and bake at 325 F for 1 1/2 hours.
  7. Every 30 minutes, break the crust that forms and push it back down, this builds the classic texture.
  8. Top with the buttered breadcrumbs and bake 20 minutes until golden.
Duck confit is pricey, good quality duck legs roasted until tender make a fine substitute.
