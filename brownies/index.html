Brownies

Deep, dark, fudgy brownies with crackly tops and molten centers. One bowl, no fuss, gone in minutes.

Prep 10 minsCook 30 minsServes 12
#American#Desserts

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Brownies, in 5 ideas

  1. Pull them early. Residual heat finishes the job, and underbaked centers are what make brownies fudgy instead of cakey.
  2. Dutch-process cocoa gives a darker color and smoother chocolate flavor than natural cocoa.
  3. Brown the butter first for a nutty depth that makes people ask what your secret is.
  4. Chill the cut brownies for clean slices, then serve at room temperature.
  5. A pinch of flaky sea salt on top balances the sweetness perfectly.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter
  • 8 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1 1/2 cups sugar
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/3 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup chocolate chips (optional)

How to make it

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 350F. Line an 8x8 inch pan with parchment paper.
  2. Melt the butter and chopped chocolate together in a heatproof bowl over simmering water, stirring until smooth. Let cool slightly.
  3. Whisk in the sugar, then the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla.
  4. Fold in the flour, cocoa powder, and salt just until no streaks remain. Stir in chocolate chips if using.
  5. Spread in the pan and bake 28 to 32 minutes, until the edges are set but the center still looks slightly underdone.
  6. Cool completely in the pan before cutting; they firm up as they cool.
Money saver. Store-brand chocolate and cocoa work just as well as premium here; the butter and technique matter more than the label.
