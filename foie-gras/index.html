Foie Gras

Seared foie gras with a crisp crust and molten center, served with something sweet and acidic. Pure luxury on a plate.

Prep 15 minsCook 10 minsServes 4
#French#Starters

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Foie Gras, in 5 ideas

  1. The pan must be nearly smoking; foie gras renders fast and needs an instant sear.
  2. Ninety seconds per side is enough; the center should be just warm and melting.
  3. Cold foie gras slices sear better than room temperature ones.
  4. Sweet and acidic accompaniments (fig jam, balsamic, port) cut the richness perfectly.
  5. Save the rendered fat; roast potatoes in it for the best potatoes of your life.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 lb foie gras, cut into 4 thick slices
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1/4 cup balsamic vinegar
  • 1/2 cup port wine
  • Toasted brioche, for serving
  • Flaky sea salt

How to make it

Directions

  1. Score the foie gras slices lightly in a crosshatch and season with salt and pepper. Chill until cooking.
  2. Sear in a dry screaming-hot skillet, 90 seconds per side, until deeply browned. Drain on paper towels.
  3. Pour off the rendered fat (save it). Add sugar to the pan and caramelize to amber.
  4. Deglaze with balsamic and port; reduce to a syrupy glaze, about 3 minutes.
  5. Plate the foie gras on toasted brioche, spoon over the glaze, and finish with flaky salt.
Money saver. Buy foie gras from a reputable source and check local regulations. Slices from the thick end of the lobe sear most evenly.
