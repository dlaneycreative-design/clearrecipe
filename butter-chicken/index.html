Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani)

Tender yogurt marinated chicken in a silky tomato, butter, and fenugreek sauce. The world's most famous Indian curry.

Prep 20 mins plus 1 hr marinatingCook 30 minsServes 4

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Butter Chicken (Murgh Makhani), in 5 ideas

  1. The char on the chicken is the flavor. Grill or broil it first. Do not skip this.
  2. Fenugreek is the signature. It is the bittersweet note that makes it taste like the restaurant.
  3. Blend the sauce. Silky is the whole point of this dish.
  4. Marinate longer than you think. An hour minimum, overnight if you can.
  5. Thighs, not breast. They stay tender through the simmer.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 500g skinless boneless chicken thighs
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 to 2 tsp hot chilli powder
  • 200g natural yogurt
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 green chilli, deseeded and finely chopped (optional)
  • 1 thumb sized piece ginger, grated
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tsp ground fenugreek
  • 3 tbsp tomato puree
  • 300ml chicken stock
  • 50g flaked almonds, toasted
  • To serve: basmati rice, naan bread, mango chutney or lime pickle, fresh coriander, lime wedges

How to make it

Directions

  1. Mix the lemon juice, cumin, paprika, chilli powder, and yogurt. Coat the chicken thoroughly, cover, and marinate at least 1 hour in the fridge (overnight is better).
  2. Grill or broil the chicken until charred at the edges, about 8 minutes. It does not need to be cooked through. Set aside.
  3. In a large pan, soften the onion in the oil over medium heat, about 8 minutes. Add the garlic, green chilli, ginger, garam masala, and fenugreek. Cook 2 minutes until fragrant.
  4. Stir in the tomato puree and stock. Simmer 15 minutes, then blend with a stick blender until completely silky.
  5. Return the sauce to the pan, add the chicken, and simmer 10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and tender.
  6. Serve with basmati rice and warm naan. Top with toasted almonds, coriander, and a squeeze of lime.
No stick blender, no problem. A regular blender works, just vent the lid and blend in batches. Hot sauce in a sealed blender is a small volcano.
