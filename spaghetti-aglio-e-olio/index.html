Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

Spaghetti slicked with garlic-infused olive oil and a pinch of chili heat. Five ingredients, twenty minutes, and somehow always more than the sum of its parts.

Prep 10 minsCook 15 minsServes 4
#Italian#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Spaghetti Aglio e Olio, in 5 ideas

  1. Low heat is the whole technique here, garlic that browns too fast goes bitter and there is no hiding it.
  2. The starchy pasta water is what turns plain oil into an actual sauce, skip it and the dish falls flat.
  3. Slice the garlic evenly so it all colors at the same rate, thick pieces burn while thin ones are still raw.
  4. Since there are only five ingredients, use the best olive oil you own, its flavor carries the dish.
  5. Traditionalists skip the cheese, but a light dusting of Parmesan never hurt anyone.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 12 oz spaghetti
  • 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 8 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 cup chopped fresh parsley
  • Salt to taste
  • Freshly grated Parmesan, optional

How to make it

Directions

  1. Boil the spaghetti in generously salted water until just al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water, then drain.
  2. While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat. Add the sliced garlic and cook slowly, stirring, until pale gold, about 3 to 4 minutes.
  3. Add the red pepper flakes and cook 30 seconds more.
  4. Ladle in 1/2 cup of the hot pasta water and swirl until the oil and water emulsify into a light sauce.
  5. Add the drained spaghetti and toss for 2 minutes so the pasta finishes cooking in the sauce and absorbs the garlic flavor.
  6. Off the heat, fold in the parsley and a pinch of salt. Serve immediately, with Parmesan if you like.
This is the ultimate pantry-cleanout pasta, so always keep good olive oil, garlic, and dried chilies on hand and dinner is never more than a box of spaghetti away.
