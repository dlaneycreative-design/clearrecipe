Porchetta

Herb rolled pork belly with shatteringly crisp skin, fennel, garlic, and rosemary. The king of Italian roasts.

Prep 40 mins plus overnight saltingCook 3 hrsServes 10
#Italian#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Porchetta, in 5 ideas

  1. Drying the skin overnight is what makes it crackle.
  2. Score the skin deeply but do not cut into the meat.
  3. Tie it snugly so the roll holds together while roasting.
  4. Watch closely during the final high heat blast, skin goes from crisp to burnt fast.
  5. Serve on crusty rolls with the pan juices for porchetta sandwiches.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 pork belly (about 5 lbs), skin on
  • 2 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp black pepper
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp fennel seeds, toasted and crushed
  • 2 tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped
  • 2 tbsp fresh sage, chopped
  • 1 tbsp lemon zest
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • Kitchen twine

How to make it

Directions

  1. Score the pork skin in a crosshatch pattern, cutting just through the skin.
  2. Rub the salt all over, especially into the skin, and refrigerate uncovered overnight to dry the skin.
  3. Mix the garlic, fennel, rosemary, sage, lemon zest, pepper, and olive oil into a paste.
  4. Rub the herb paste over the meat side of the belly.
  5. Roll the belly up tightly from a long edge and tie with kitchen twine every 2 inches.
  6. Let it come to room temperature 1 hour before roasting.
  7. Roast at 325 F for 2 1/2 hours, then raise to 450 F for 30 minutes to crisp the skin.
  8. Rest 20 minutes before removing twine and slicing thick.
Ask the butcher for a pork belly with even thickness so it rolls and cooks uniformly.
