Naan Bread

Pillowy, blistered flatbread with chewy edges, the perfect scoop for every curry.

Prep 20 mins plus risingCook 15 minsServes 8
#Indian#Sides

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Naan Bread, in 5 ideas

  1. A ripping hot pan mimics the tandoor blast.
  2. Do not roll too thin; thickness gives the chew.
  3. Brush with garlic butter for garlic naan.
  4. Keep cooked naan wrapped in a towel so they stay soft.
  5. Freeze extras with parchment between them.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all purpose flour
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp instant yeast
  • 3/4 cup warm yogurt
  • 1/4 cup warm water
  • 2 tbsp melted butter, plus more for brushing
  • 1 tsp nigella seeds (optional)

How to make it

Directions

  1. Mix flour, sugar, salt, and yeast in a large bowl.
  2. Add yogurt, water, and butter; knead 8 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  3. Cover and rise 1 hour until doubled.
  4. Divide into 8 balls and roll into teardrops.
  5. Cook on a screaming hot cast iron skillet until puffed and charred in spots, about 2 minutes per side.
  6. Brush with butter and sprinkle with nigella seeds.
Money saver. A cast iron skillet works great; no tandoor or special equipment needed.
