Fajitas

Sizzling strips of marinated steak or chicken with charred peppers and onions, served with warm tortillas. Dinner theater on a platter.

Prep 15 mins plus marinadeCook 15 minsServes 4
#Mexican#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Fajitas, in 5 ideas

  1. Skirt steak is the traditional cut; its loose grain soaks up marinade
  2. Slicing against the grain is what makes fajita meat tender instead of chewy
  3. A screaming hot pan gives the char without steaming the vegetables
  4. The sizzle is showmanship, but the char is flavor
  5. Leftover fajita mix makes incredible quesadillas the next day

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs skirt or flank steak, or chicken breast
  • 1/4 cup lime juice
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp cumin
  • 1 tbsp chili powder
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 12 flour tortillas
  • Guacamole, sour cream, and salsa, for serving

How to make it

Directions

  1. Marinate the meat in lime juice, oil, garlic, cumin, and chili powder for 1 to 4 hours.
  2. Sear the meat in a ripping hot skillet 3 minutes per side. Rest and slice against the grain.
  3. In the same skillet, char the peppers and onions until softened with blackened edges.
  4. Return the sliced meat to the pan and toss everything together.
  5. Serve sizzling with warm tortillas and all the fixings.
Money saver. Skirt steak is often cheaper than flank and has better flavor for fajitas; ask the butcher for the outside skirt.
