Terrine

Layered vegetable and herb terrine set in aspic or simply pressed: a mosaic of color that slices like a jewel.

Prep 40 minsCook 30 minsServes 8

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Terrine, in 5 ideas

  1. The overnight weighted chill is what makes it slice cleanly instead of crumbling.
  2. Blanch each vegetable just until crisp-tender; they cook more in the oven.
  3. Think in color layers: green, orange, red, green makes a stunning cross-section.
  4. A sharp thin knife wiped clean between cuts gives the prettiest slices.
  5. Serve with a simple vinaigrette-dressed green salad.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 2 zucchini, sliced lengthwise
  • 2 red bell peppers, roasted and peeled
  • 1 lb spinach, blanched
  • 3 carrots, sliced lengthwise and blanched
  • 8 eggs, beaten
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup grated parmesan
  • Salt, pepper, and nutmeg
  • Olive oil

How to make it

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 375F. Oil a terrine or loaf pan and line with parchment.
  2. Whisk the eggs, cream, parmesan, salt, pepper, and a grating of nutmeg.
  3. Layer the vegetables in the pan, pouring a little egg mixture between each layer.
  4. Pour the remaining egg mixture over the top and press gently.
  5. Bake in a water bath 45 minutes until set and golden.
  6. Cool completely, chill overnight weighted, then unmold and slice.
Money saver. Roast the peppers yourself over a gas flame or under the broiler. Jarred peppers work but lack the smoky depth.
