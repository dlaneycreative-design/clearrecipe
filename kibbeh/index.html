Kibbeh

A baked casserole of spiced bulgur and ground meat with a savory lamb and pine nut filling.

Prep 40 minsCook 45 minsServes 8

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Kibbeh, in 5 ideas

  1. Squeezing the bulgur dry is critical for a shell that holds.
  2. Wet hands make spreading the shell much easier.
  3. Toasted pine nuts add richness to the filling.
  4. Scoring before baking gives clean serving pieces.
  5. Serve with yogurt and a simple salad.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 2 cups fine bulgur
  • 1.5 lbs ground lamb, divided
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp allspice
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1/2 cup pine nuts, toasted
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon

How to make it

Directions

  1. Soak bulgur 20 minutes; squeeze very dry.
  2. Blend half the lamb with grated onion and spices into a smooth paste; mix with bulgur to form the shell.
  3. Saute diced onion, remaining lamb, pine nuts, and cinnamon for the filling.
  4. Press half the shell mixture into an oiled baking dish.
  5. Spread the filling evenly, then top with remaining shell mixture.
  6. Score the top in diamonds and drizzle with olive oil.
  7. Bake at 375F for 40 minutes until golden and firm.
Money saver. Make it in a big batch; it reheats wonderfully for days.
