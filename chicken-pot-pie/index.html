Chicken Pot Pie

Tender chicken and vegetables in a creamy gravy under a flaky golden crust. The ultimate comfort food, made from scratch.

Prep 25 minsCook 45 minsServes 6
#American#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Chicken Pot Pie, in 5 ideas

  1. Chicken thighs stay juicier than breast in the creamy filling.
  2. Cooking the flour in butter for 2 minutes removes the raw flour taste.
  3. Vent the top crust or the steam makes it soggy from underneath.
  4. An egg wash on the top crust gives a beautiful glossy finish.
  5. Let it rest before cutting so the filling sets instead of running everywhere.

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 lb boneless chicken thighs, cut into chunks
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1/3 cup flour
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 package refrigerated pie crusts (2 crusts)

How to make it

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 400F.
  2. Melt butter in a large skillet. Cook the onion, carrots, and celery 8 minutes until softened.
  3. Add the chicken and cook 5 minutes until no longer pink.
  4. Stir in the flour and cook 2 minutes. Slowly whisk in the broth, then the cream and thyme. Simmer until thick, about 5 minutes. Stir in the peas. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Line a pie dish with one crust. Pour in the filling.
  6. Top with the second crust, crimp the edges, and cut vents. Bake 35 to 40 minutes until deep golden. Cool 10 minutes before serving.
Use a rotisserie chicken and store bought crust to cut the active time in half. Nobody will know the difference.
