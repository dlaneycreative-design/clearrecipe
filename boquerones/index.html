Boquerones

White anchovies marinated in vinegar with garlic and parsley. Bright, briny, and nothing like the canned brown kind.

Prep 15 mins plus marinateCook 0 minsServes 4
#Spanish#Mains

The whole recipe in 60 seconds

Boquerones, in 5 ideas

  1. The vinegar essentially cures the fish, like a ceviche
  2. White anchovies are mild and delicate, a world away from canned
  3. This is a staple tapa in every Spanish bar
  4. Good olive oil matters here since it is a main flavor
  5. They keep a week in the fridge under oil

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 lb fresh white anchovies, cleaned and filleted
  • 1 cup white wine vinegar
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup parsley, chopped
  • Salt

How to make it

Directions

  1. Layer the anchovy fillets in a dish and cover with vinegar and salt.
  2. Refrigerate 4 hours until the flesh turns white and firm.
  3. Drain the vinegar.
  4. Cover with olive oil, garlic, and parsley.
  5. Marinate 1 more hour and serve cold with bread.
Money saver. Buy them pre cleaned from a fishmonger; cleaning tiny anchovies yourself is tedious work.
